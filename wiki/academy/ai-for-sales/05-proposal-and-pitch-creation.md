  # Proposal & Pitch Creation

  Build compelling, customized proposals in minutes — not days.


  ### What You'll Learn


    - How to generate tailored proposals with AI in under 30 minutes

    - The proposal structure that wins more deals

    - Pitch deck outlines and executive summaries on demand




  The Problem
  ## Proposals Are a Time Trap

  Most salespeople dread proposals. They take hours to write, they're often copy-pasted from old ones, and they rarely speak directly to the prospect's specific situation. That's why most proposals lose — they're generic documents dressed up with a new logo.
  AI turns proposal creation from a half-day chore into a 30-minute sprint. More importantly, it makes every proposal genuinely customized because you can feed it everything you learned from your discovery calls.


  Framework
  ## The Winning Proposal Structure

  Every great proposal follows this arc — it mirrors how buyers actually make decisions:

    **1. Their Problem:** Show you understand their pain better than they do
    **2. Cost of Inaction:** What happens if they don't solve this? Make it real.
    **3. Your Solution:** Mapped specifically to their problems (not a feature list)
    **4. Proof:** Case studies, results, testimonials from similar companies
    **5. Investment & ROI:** Frame price as investment. Show the math.
    **6. Next Steps:** Make saying yes easy. Remove friction.



  ### Try It Now

  Generate a proposal outline from your call notes:
  `Create a proposal outline for [COMPANY]. They need [WHAT THEY NEED]. Their main pain points from our calls: [LIST PAINS]. Our solution: [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Pricing: [YOUR PRICING]. Write each section with specific language tailored to their situation. Include a compelling executive summary (3 sentences max) and an ROI calculation showing the value vs. cost. Tone: confident, not pushy.`


  Pitch Decks
  ## AI-Generated Pitch Outlines

  Need a pitch deck for a presentation? AI can outline every slide with talking points. You handle the design and delivery — AI handles the structure and messaging.
  The key is feeding AI your discovery notes so the pitch speaks directly to what the prospect cares about. A pitch that opens with their specific challenge and ends with their specific ROI wins every time.


  ### Build a Pitch Deck Outline

  `Create a 10-slide pitch deck outline for presenting to [COMPANY]. Their key decision-makers: [NAMES/ROLES]. Their challenges: [LIST THEM]. For each slide, give me: the headline, 3 bullet points, and talking notes. Focus on their ROI, not our features. End with a clear call to action.`


  Templates
  ## Proposal Templates by Deal Type

  Different deals need different proposal approaches. AI can generate the right format for each situation when you specify the context:

    **Small Deal / Quick Close:** A 1-2 page proposal. Executive summary, 3 pain points mapped to your solution, pricing, and a single next step. No fluff. Speed and clarity win small deals.
    **Mid-Market / Multiple Stakeholders:** A 3-5 page proposal with an executive summary for the decision-maker, a detailed section for the evaluator, ROI calculations for the CFO, and implementation timeline for the project manager. Each section speaks to a different reader.
    **Enterprise / RFP Response:** A comprehensive document structured around their requirements. AI maps your capabilities to each requirement, generates compliance matrices, and drafts differentiator sections. This is where AI saves days of work.
    **Renewal / Upsell:** A results-focused proposal. Lead with what you have already delivered — metrics, outcomes, milestones. Then show how the expansion builds on proven success. Renewals sell on track record, not promises.



  ### Multi-Stakeholder Proposal

  Build a proposal that speaks to every reader:
  `Create a proposal for [COMPANY] with sections tailored to multiple stakeholders. Decision-maker: [NAME, TITLE] — cares about ROI and strategic alignment. Evaluator: [NAME, TITLE] — cares about features and integration. Finance: needs an ROI model and cost breakdown. Our product: [PRODUCT]. Their pain points: [LIST]. Pricing: [PRICING]. Write each section in the language that reader cares about. Include an executive summary that works as a standalone document.`


  Pitch Strategy
  ## Pitch Deck Strategies That Win Rooms

  A pitch deck is not a document you read aloud. It is a visual aid for a conversation. AI helps you build decks that support your story without replacing it:

    **The Problem-Solution Arc:** Open with their world (the problem), show the gap (the cost of inaction), reveal the bridge (your solution), and land on the destination (their future with you). Every great pitch follows this arc.
    **The Rule of Three:** No more than three bullet points per slide. No more than three key messages per section. Human brains process information in threes. AI can distill complex ideas into three-point summaries perfectly.
    **The Proof Sandwich:** Before any claim, set it up with context. After every claim, back it with proof — a number, a case study, a testimonial. AI can generate these proof sandwiches for every slide in your deck.

  Ask AI to critique your pitch deck outline before you build it. Feed it the outline and your prospect's profile, and ask: "What would a skeptical [TITLE] challenge in this pitch? Where is my argument weakest?" Fix the weaknesses before you walk into the room.


  Executive Summaries
  ## The Art of the Executive Summary

  Many decision-makers read only the executive summary. If your entire proposal had to be reduced to three sentences, could it still win the deal? That is the standard your executive summary must meet.
  AI excels at distillation. Feed it your full proposal and ask for a three-sentence executive summary that answers: What is the problem? What is the solution? What is the expected outcome? Then ask for a one-paragraph version for stakeholders who will read slightly more. Having both versions gives you flexibility in how you present.

    **Bad Executive Summary:** "We are pleased to present this proposal for your consideration. Our company has been a leader in this space for 15 years. We believe our solution is a strong fit." — This says nothing about their problem or the value you deliver.
    **Good Executive Summary:** "[Company] is losing $500K annually to manual data entry errors. Our automated validation system eliminates 95% of these errors within 60 days of implementation. The projected ROI is 340% in the first year." — Three sentences. Problem, solution, outcome.



  ROI Models
  ## Building ROI Models with AI

  The most persuasive part of any proposal is the ROI model.
  When a buyer can see the math — when the investment clearly pays for itself —
  price objections disappear. AI builds these models quickly from basic inputs:

    **Cost of the Problem:**
    How much is the current problem costing them? AI calculates this from data
    points like hours wasted, error rates, missed revenue, and employee turnover.
    Make the cost of inaction a concrete number, not a vague feeling.
    **Value of the Solution:**
    What specific outcomes does your product deliver? Time saved, revenue gained,
    costs reduced, risks eliminated. Map each outcome to a dollar value using
    their company's actual numbers when possible.
    **Time to Value:**
    How quickly do they see results? A solution that pays for itself in 90 days
    is an easier yes than one that takes 18 months. AI can model the timeline
    based on similar customer implementations.
    **Total ROI:**
    Value of the solution minus cost of the solution, expressed as a percentage
    and a payback period. A 3x ROI in year one is compelling. A 10x ROI is
    undeniable. AI calculates these from the inputs above.



  ### ROI Calculator Prompt

  `Build an ROI model for [COMPANY]. Their problem:
[DESCRIBE THE PROBLEM]. Current cost of the problem: [ESTIMATED ANNUAL COST
OR "help me estimate"]. My solution: [YOUR PRODUCT] at [YOUR PRICE].
Expected outcomes: [LIST EXPECTED IMPROVEMENTS]. Calculate: 1) Annual cost
savings, 2) Revenue impact, 3) Total ROI percentage, 4) Payback period in
months, 5) A one-paragraph ROI summary I can paste into the proposal.
Use conservative estimates — understating ROI builds more trust than
overstating it.`


  Mistakes
  ## Proposal Mistakes That Kill Deals

  Even great proposals fail when they contain these
  common errors. AI helps you avoid each one:

    **Mistake 1 — Leading with Features:**
    Starting the proposal with what your product does instead of what the
    customer needs. AI reorders your proposal to lead with their pain
    and end with your solution.
    **Mistake 2 — Generic Case Studies:**
    Including case studies from unrelated industries. AI selects or adapts
    examples that match the prospect's industry, size, and use case.
    **Mistake 3 — Burying the Price:**
    Hiding pricing on page 12 signals you are afraid of your own numbers.
    AI frames pricing as investment and positions it after the ROI model
    so the value is established before the number appears.
    **Mistake 4 — No Clear Next Step:**
    Ending with "let us know if you have questions." AI closes every proposal
    with a specific next step, a specific date, and a specific person
    responsible. Ambiguity kills momentum.



  Speed Wins
  ## The Fast-Proposal Advantage

  Deals have momentum. Every day between "send me a proposal" and actually sending it, your close rate drops. AI lets you send a polished, customized proposal the same day — often within hours of the call. That speed signals competence, eagerness, and professionalism. It also beats your slower competitors to the finish line.

  Review
  ## Review the winning proposal structure.


### Winning Proposal Structure

**Card 1:**
Front: 1. Their Problem
Back: Show you understand their pain better than they do. This is the foundation — if you get this wrong, nothing else matters.

**Card 2:**
Front: 2. Cost of Inaction
Back: What happens if they do not solve this? Make it real with specifics. Urgency comes from the cost of waiting, not from pressure.

**Card 3:**
Front: 3. Your Solution
Back: Mapped specifically to their problems — not a feature list. Each capability connects to a pain they told you about.

**Card 4:**
Front: 4. Proof
Back: Case studies, results, and testimonials from similar companies. Social proof at the right moment removes doubt.

**Card 5:**
Front: 5. Investment and ROI
Back: Frame price as investment. Show the math. Make the value concrete before revealing the cost.

**Card 6:**
Front: 6. Next Steps
Back: Make saying yes easy. Remove friction. A clear, simple next action closes more deals than a persuasive argument.
