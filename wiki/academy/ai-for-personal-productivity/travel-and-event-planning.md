  # Travel and Event Planning.

  Plan trips, parties, and gatherings without the overwhelm. AI handles the logistics.


  ### After this lesson you'll know


    - How to build a complete trip itinerary with one conversation

    - The "constraint-first" method for realistic travel planning

    - How to plan events, parties, and gatherings with AI as your coordinator

    - Packing lists, checklists, and timelines generated instantly




  Why This Works
  ## Planning is just organized decision-making. AI is built for this.

  Planning a trip or event involves hundreds of small decisions: where to stay, what to do, when to do it, how to get there, what to bring. Each decision is simple on its own, but together they're overwhelming.
  AI doesn't get overwhelmed. It takes your constraints (budget, dates, preferences, group size) and builds a complete plan. You review, adjust, and go. The hours you'd spend on travel blogs and Pinterest boards become a 10-minute conversation.


  Travel Planning
  ## Constraints first, then let AI build the dream.


    The more constraints you give, the more realistic and useful the plan. Include:

      Example travel prompt:
      `"Plan a 4-day trip to Nashville for 2 people. Budget: $1,500 total including hotel. We love live music, good food, and walkable neighborhoods. We don't want a packed schedule — leave room to wander. We're driving from Atlanta. Give me a day-by-day itinerary with restaurant suggestions and one backup plan for rain."`

    AI will build a realistic itinerary with morning, afternoon, and evening activities, restaurant picks near each activity, drive time estimates, and that rain backup. Then you can say "swap Day 2 lunch for something cheaper" or "add a brewery tour" and it adjusts.



  Event Planning
  ## Birthdays, dinners, reunions — AI is your event coordinator.



      Birthday party
      "I'm planning my daughter's 7th birthday party. 12 kids. Budget: $200. We have a backyard. Give me a theme, activities for 2 hours, a simple food menu, and a timeline so I know when to do what."


      Dinner party
      "I'm hosting 6 friends for dinner Saturday. One is vegetarian, one is gluten-free. I'm an intermediate cook. Give me a menu, a prep timeline starting Friday night, and a grocery list."


      Family reunion
      "Help me plan a family reunion for 30 people at a park. I need a potluck sign-up system, activities for ages 5-75, and a day-of schedule. Budget: $300 for shared supplies."




  Bonus Tools
  ## Packing lists, checklists, and countdowns.

  Once your plan is set, AI generates everything else you need. Ask for a packing list customized to your destination and weather. Ask for a pre-trip checklist (stop the mail, set light timers, charge devices). Ask for a countdown timeline: "What should I do 2 weeks before, 1 week before, and the day before?"
  These lists take AI about 10 seconds to generate and would take you 30 minutes to think through on your own. That's the trade you're making every time you use AI for planning: your mental energy for its processing speed.


  Planning Checklists
  ## Pre-trip and pre-event checklists you can generate instantly.

  One of the most underrated uses of AI for planning: generating the checklists you didn't know you needed. These prompts create comprehensive checklists customized to your specific trip or event.


      Pre-trip countdown checklist

        `"I'm leaving for a 5-day trip to [DESTINATION] in 2 weeks. Give me a countdown checklist: what to do 2 weeks before, 1 week before, 3 days before, the day before, and morning of departure. Include things like mail, pets, plants, house security, and documents."`



      Weather-smart packing list

        `"I'm going to Portland, Oregon in November for 4 days. I'll be doing a mix of city walking and one day hike. Generate a packing list that accounts for rain, layering, and casual dining. I want to pack carry-on only."`



      Event day-of checklist

        `"I'm hosting a backyard BBQ for 20 people this Saturday starting at 4pm. Give me a day-of timeline starting at 10am: when to start marinating, when to set up chairs, when to start the grill, when to put out drinks. Include a backup plan if it rains."`





  Travel Research
  ## Prompt templates for researching any destination.

  Before you build an itinerary, sometimes you need to research the destination itself. These prompts help you learn about a place quickly and make informed decisions about where to go and what to do.


      Destination comparison
      "I have a week off in March and a $2,000 budget for 2 people. I'm considering Savannah, Asheville, or San Antonio. Compare all three: average March weather, estimated costs (hotel + food + activities), best for food lovers, and walkability. Help me decide."


      Neighborhood guide
      "I'm visiting Chicago for 3 days. Explain the main neighborhoods tourists should know about: which ones are walkable, which have the best food, which are best for nightlife, and which I should book my hotel in if I want to walk to everything."


      Local tips request
      "What do locals in Austin, Texas wish tourists knew? Give me 10 tips about getting around, where to eat that isn't on every tourist list, cultural norms, tipping expectations, and common mistakes visitors make."


      Accessibility planning
      "I use a wheelchair and I'm planning a trip to San Francisco. What should I know about accessibility — public transit, hilly terrain, restaurant seating, hotel considerations? What neighborhoods are most wheelchair-friendly?"




  Group Planning
  ## Managing group trips and events without losing your mind.

  Planning for a group multiplies every decision. AI helps you manage different preferences, dietary needs, budget levels, and energy levels across the entire group.


      The group trip prompt
      "I'm planning a bachelorette weekend for 8 women in Nashville. Budget ranges from $200-400 per person. Ages 25-40. Half the group are big drinkers, half prefer daytime activities. Two are vegetarian. Plan a 2-day itinerary that keeps everyone happy without splitting the group too much."


      The potluck organizer
      "I'm organizing a potluck for 15 people. 3 are vegetarian, 1 is gluten-free, 2 have nut allergies. Help me create a sign-up list with categories (mains, sides, desserts, drinks) and suggest specific dishes for each category that accommodate everyone's needs."




  Try It
  ## Plan a trip or event.


    Pick whichever is more relevant to your life right now:

      `Option A (trip): "Plan a [LENGTH] trip to [DESTINATION] for [NUMBER] people. Budget: $[AMOUNT]. We like [INTERESTS]. We don't like [DISLIKES]. Give me a day-by-day itinerary with food suggestions."`


      `Option B (event): "Help me plan a [EVENT TYPE] for [NUMBER] people. Budget: $[AMOUNT]. Location: [WHERE]. Give me a timeline, menu ideas, and a to-do list counting down from 2 weeks out."`

    After you get the plan, try: "Generate a packing list for this trip" or "What am I forgetting?" AI is surprisingly good at catching things you'd miss.



  Important Note
  ## Always verify bookings and reservations yourself.

  AI is exceptional at planning and organizing travel, but it cannot make actual bookings for you. Use AI to build the plan, compare options, and generate checklists — then confirm all reservations, hours, and availability through official websites or by calling directly.
  Prices, availability, and business hours change constantly. AI's suggestions are an excellent starting point, not confirmed reservations. This is especially important for restaurants with limited seating, tours that sell out, and accommodation during peak travel seasons.
  The ideal workflow: AI plans, you verify, you book. This combination gives you the best of both worlds — AI's comprehensive planning ability and your real-time confirmation.
  Pro tip: after your trip, tell AI what worked and what didn't. "The restaurant on Day 2 was closed" or "we needed more downtime between activities." This feedback makes your next trip plan even better.
