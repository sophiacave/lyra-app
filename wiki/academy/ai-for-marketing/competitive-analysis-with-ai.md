  # Competitive Analysis with AI.

  Know your market, spot gaps, and position yourself — all with AI-powered research.


  ### After this lesson you'll know


    - How to analyze competitors' messaging and positioning with AI

    - Finding market gaps and opportunities your competitors miss

    - Building a competitive matrix in minutes

    - Turning competitive insights into marketing advantage




  Messaging Analysis
  ## Deconstruct any competitor's messaging in 5 minutes.

  Copy the text from a competitor's homepage, landing page, or about page and paste it into AI with this prompt:


    Analyze this competitor's marketing messaging:

[Paste their homepage/landing page text here]

Tell me:
1. Who is their target audience? (Be specific)
2. What's their primary value proposition?
3. What emotional triggers are they using?
4. What objections are they addressing?
5. What's their tone/voice? (formal, casual, technical, etc.)
6. What claims are they making? (With evidence or without?)
7. What's MISSING from their messaging?

Then: Based on this analysis, how should I position
MY product ([describe yours]) to differentiate clearly?



  The Matrix
  ## Build a competitive matrix instantly.



    Create a competitive analysis matrix.

My product: [Name + what it does + price]
Competitors:
1. [Competitor A — what they do, price point]
2. [Competitor B — what they do, price point]
3. [Competitor C — what they do, price point]

Create a comparison table with columns:
- Feature/Aspect
- My Product
- Competitor A
- Competitor B
- Competitor C

Include rows for:
- Core features
- Pricing/value
- Target audience
- Ease of use
- Customer support
- Unique differentiator
- Biggest weakness

Then give me:
- 3 areas where I have a clear advantage
- 3 areas where competitors beat me
- 3 gaps in the market nobody is filling



  Gap Finding
  ## Find the opportunities everyone else misses.

  The most valuable competitive intelligence isn't about what competitors are doing — it's about what they're NOT doing:



      → "Based on these competitor analyses, what audiences are being underserved?"
      → "What pain points do customers have that none of these competitors address in their messaging?"
      → "What content topics are competitors ranking for that I'm not covering?"
      → "If I could only compete on ONE dimension, which gives me the biggest advantage?"




  Action
  ## Turn insights into messaging.

  Competitive analysis is worthless unless it changes what you do. After analysis, ask AI:


    Based on this competitive analysis, write me:

1. A positioning statement (one sentence that captures
   why someone should choose me over alternatives)
2. 3 "why us" bullet points for my landing page
3. A comparison page outline (us vs competitors —
   honest, not trash-talking)
4. 5 objection-handling responses for sales conversations
   ("Why should I choose you over [Competitor]?")

  This turns hours of strategic work into a 15-minute AI-assisted session that produces actionable marketing assets.


  SWOT Analysis
  ## AI-powered SWOT analysis in 10 minutes.

  The classic SWOT framework becomes dramatically more useful when you feed AI your competitive data and let it map the landscape:


    Based on my competitive analysis, create a SWOT analysis
for my business:

My product: [Name + what it does]
Competitors analyzed: [List competitors you researched]
Key findings: [Paste your competitive matrix or notes]

For each quadrant, give me:
- Strengths: 5 internal advantages I have over competitors
- Weaknesses: 5 internal areas where competitors beat me
- Opportunities: 5 external trends or gaps I can exploit
- Threats: 5 external risks from competitors or market changes

Then:
For each item, suggest ONE specific marketing action I can take.
Not vague strategy — specific copy, campaigns, or content I should create.

Be brutally honest. A SWOT that only shows strengths is useless.



  Monitoring
  ## Set up ongoing competitor monitoring with AI.

  Competitive analysis isn't a one-time event. The best marketers monitor their competitors continuously. Here's a monthly competitor check-in prompt you can run every 30 days:



      Website changes: Visit your top 3 competitors' homepages monthly. Paste the new copy into AI alongside the old copy. Ask: "What changed in their messaging? What does that suggest about their strategy?"
      Content tracking: Subscribe to competitors' newsletters and follow their social accounts. Monthly, paste their recent content into AI and ask: "What topics are they focusing on? What content gaps remain?"
      Review mining: Read competitors' latest reviews on G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot. Paste them into AI and ask: "What are customers complaining about? What pain points can I address in my marketing?"
      Pricing intel: Check competitors' pricing pages quarterly. Ask AI: "Compare these pricing models. Where is there a pricing gap I could fill? Are competitors moving upmarket or downmarket?"
      New entrant watch: Ask AI: "Based on my industry ([describe it]), what types of new competitors should I watch for? What adjacent markets could produce a disruptor?"


  **The discipline:** Block 30 minutes on the first Monday of every month for competitor check-in. Use the prompts above. Update your competitive matrix. The marketers who track their competitive landscape monthly will never be surprised by a competitor's move.


    Monthly Competitor Check-in Prompt:

Here are the latest updates from my top 3 competitors:
[Paste recent changes, content, or announcements]

Compare to their previous positioning:
[Paste or summarize what they used to say/do]

Tell me:
1. What strategic shifts are they making?
2. Are they moving toward or away from my positioning?
3. Any new gaps opening up that I should exploit?
4. What should I adjust in my marketing this month?

Be specific. Give me actions, not observations.
