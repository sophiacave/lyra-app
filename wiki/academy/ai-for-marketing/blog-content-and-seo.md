  # Blog Content and SEO.

  Topic Research
  ## Find topics your audience actually searches for.

  Instead of guessing what to write about, use AI to generate topic clusters based on your business:


    I run a [type of business] targeting [audience].

Generate 20 blog post ideas organized into 4 topic clusters.
Each cluster should have a pillar post (comprehensive, 2000+ words)
and 4 supporting posts (focused, 800-1200 words) that link to it.

For each post idea:
- Suggested title (SEO-friendly, under 60 characters)
- Target keyword phrase
- Search intent (informational/commercial/navigational)
- Estimated difficulty (low/medium/high competition)

Focus on: Topics where someone searching would be a potential customer.
Skip purely educational topics that attract the wrong audience.



  Writing
  ## The blog post prompt that gets you 80% there.



    Write a blog post:

Title: [Your SEO-optimized title]
Target keyword: [Primary keyword phrase]
Word count: [800-2000 depending on topic depth]
Audience: [Who's reading and what they know]

Structure:
- Hook opening (NOT "In today's digital age...")
- Clear H2 subheadings (include keyword variations naturally)
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Practical examples or actionable steps
- Conclusion with a clear next step or CTA

SEO requirements:
- Use the target keyword in the first 100 words
- Include 3-5 related keywords naturally throughout
- Write a meta description (under 155 characters)
- Suggest 3 internal linking opportunities

Voice: [Your brand voice]
Don't: Stuff keywords, write filler paragraphs, use "in conclusion," or start any paragraph with "It's important to note that."



  SEO Optimization
  ## AI-powered SEO optimization checklist.

  After writing the post, use AI to optimize it:



      ✓ "Review this post for SEO. Is the keyword used naturally? Are the H2s optimized? Suggest improvements."
      ✓ "Write 5 title tag options for this post (under 60 characters, keyword-first)."
      ✓ "Generate alt text for 4 images I'll add to this blog post about [topic]."
      ✓ "Suggest 5 FAQ questions (with answers) I can add as an FAQ section for featured snippet potential."
      ✓ "Write a JSON-LD schema markup for this blog post."




  The Google Question
  ## How to avoid AI content penalties.

  Google's stance: they don't penalize AI content for being AI-generated. They penalize **low-quality, unhelpful content** — regardless of who or what wrote it. The rules are the same they've always been:




        Google Loves

          - Original insights and expertise

          - Content that solves real problems

          - Unique data, examples, or perspectives

          - Content written for humans, not algorithms




        Google Penalizes

          - Thin content that says nothing new

          - Mass-produced content farms

          - Keyword stuffing and SEO manipulation

          - Content that exists only to rank, not to help





  **The formula:** AI draft + your expertise + your examples + your editing = content that ranks AND helps people. Skip any of those steps and you're just adding noise to the internet.


  SEO Checklist
  ## The complete on-page SEO checklist for every blog post.

  Before you hit publish, run through this checklist. Ask AI to audit your post against each item:




        Title tag
        Under 60 characters. Keyword near the front. Compelling enough to click in search results.


        Meta description
        Under 155 characters. Includes the keyword naturally. Reads like a mini-ad that makes searchers want to click.


        URL slug
        Short, descriptive, keyword-included. Use hyphens between words. No dates, no numbers, no random characters.


        H1 heading
        One per page. Contains the primary keyword. Different from the title tag (slightly) for variety.


        H2 subheadings
        Include keyword variations naturally. Break content into scannable sections. Every 200-300 words should have a subheading.


        First 100 words
        Contains the primary keyword. Sets up the topic clearly. Hooks the reader immediately.


        Internal links
        Link to 3-5 related pages on your site. Use descriptive anchor text, not "click here."


        Image alt text
        Descriptive alt text on every image. Include keywords where natural. Helps accessibility and image search.


        Readability
        Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences). Active voice. No jargon unless your audience expects it. 8th-grade reading level.





  Keyword Strategy
  ## The AI keyword research workflow.

  You don't need expensive SEO tools to do keyword research. AI can help you build a keyword strategy from scratch:


    I run a [type of business] targeting [audience].

Help me build a keyword strategy:

Step 1: Generate 10 "seed keywords" — broad terms
my audience would search for.

Step 2: For each seed keyword, generate 5 long-tail
variations (3-5 word phrases with lower competition).

Step 3: Categorize all 60 keywords by search intent:
- Informational ("how to...", "what is...")
- Commercial ("best...", "vs...", "review...")
- Transactional ("buy...", "pricing...", "sign up...")

Then prioritize: Which 10 keywords should I target
first? Consider: relevance to my business, likely
competition level, and closeness to a purchase decision.

Format as a table: Keyword | Intent | Priority | Blog Post Idea

  **Pro tip:** Target commercial and transactional keywords first — those are people closer to buying. Informational keywords build traffic but convert less directly. A mix of both is ideal, but revenue-driving keywords come first.
