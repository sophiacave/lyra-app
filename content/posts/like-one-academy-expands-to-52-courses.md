# Like One Academy Just Expanded to 52 Courses

When we launched the Academy in March, we had 36 courses. Today we have 52.

That's 16 new courses and 160 new lessons — built in a single session, shipped the same day. Every course follows the same standard: MIT-depth instruction, Gen Z voice, hands-on exercises in every lesson.

## What's New

The expansion fills gaps we've been hearing about. Here's what we added:

**Beginner tier** (6 new courses):
- **AI Side Hustles: Your First $1K** — freelancing, micro-SaaS, print-on-demand with AI
- **AI Music Producer** — Suno, Udio, Timbre. Bedroom to Spotify
- **AI for Students** — study smarter without cheating
- **AI for Social Media Creators** — TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn
- **AI for Parents & Educators** — age-appropriate AI for kids
- **AI for Mental Wellness** — journaling, habit tracking, emotional regulation

**Intermediate tier** (7 new courses):
- **AI Freelancer's Toolkit** — win clients, deliver faster, charge more
- **AI for Finance & Budgeting** — personal finance automation
- **AI Game Dev: No Code** — build playable games by talking to AI
- **AI Personal Brand Builder** — portfolio, content strategy, monetization
- **AI for Nonprofit Impact** — fundraising, grants, impact measurement
- **Local AI & Privacy-First Computing** — Ollama, llama.cpp, zero cloud dependency
- **AI Cinema & Filmmaking** — script to screen for $2-5 per video

**Advanced tier** (3 new courses):
- **Fine-Tuning & Custom Models** — LoRA, QLoRA, RLHF, DPO
- **AI Systems Design** — architecture patterns for production AI
- **AI for Scientific Research** — hypothesis generation to paper writing

## Same Model, More Access

First 3 lessons of every course are still free. That's now 156 free lessons across 52 courses — no credit card, no signup wall.

Pro unlocks everything: all 520+ lessons, exercises, downloads, and certificates. Still $49/month. Still cancel anytime.

## Why This Matters

Most AI education platforms add courses slowly — one per quarter, maybe. We added 16 in a day because the entire Academy is built by the same AI-native system it teaches. The content pipeline, the quality gates, the deployment — it's all automated.

That's the point. We don't just teach AI. We run on it.
