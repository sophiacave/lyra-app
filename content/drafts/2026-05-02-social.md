🚀 Tired of getting irrelevant AI responses? You're not alone! Many solopreneurs and small business owners share this frustration. The key to unlocking AI’s potential lies in how you prompt it. 

Most AI prompt failures stem from miscommunication. Common pitfalls like vagueness, lack of context, and overly complex requests can leave you with generic answers that don’t serve your needs. But what if there was a way to turn this around? Enter the CRAFT framework.

CRAFT stands for Context, Relevance, Actionability, Follow-up, and Tone. By mastering these five components, you can transform your AI interactions into powerful tools that drive real results. For example, providing context about your business can lead to tailored insights that actually resonate with your objectives.
