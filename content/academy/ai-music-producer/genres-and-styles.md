  <span class="section-label">Genre DNA</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Every genre has a fingerprint. Learn it, prompt it, nail it.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">AI music generators understand genre through patterns — tempo ranges, instrument choices, rhythm structures, and tonal qualities. When you understand what makes a genre sound like itself, you can describe it precisely to AI and get authentic results.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Here are the DNA profiles of the eight most commercially viable genres:</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Genre Profiles</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Eight genres decoded with ready-to-use prompt templates.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>1. Lo-Fi Hip-Hop</strong> — BPM: 70-90. Instruments: dusty piano, vinyl crackle, soft boom-bap drums, jazz guitar samples. Mood: nostalgic, cozy, study-session. AI excels here. Prompt: "Lo-fi hip-hop, 80 BPM, jazzy piano chords, vinyl texture, side-chain compression on kick, mellow and nostalgic, late-night study vibes."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>2. Trap</strong> — BPM: 130-170. Instruments: 808 sub-bass, sharp hi-hats with rolls, snappy snares, dark synth leads. Mood: aggressive, flexing, dark. AI is strong here — 808 patterns are well-represented in training data. Prompt: "Hard trap beat, 145 BPM, heavy 808 bass slides, rapid hi-hat rolls, dark minor key melody, aggressive energy, Lex Luger style."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>3. Pop</strong> — BPM: 100-130. Instruments: clean synths, claps, four-on-the-floor kick pattern, bright vocals, layered harmonies. Mood: uplifting, catchy, anthemic. AI does well with structure but can sound generic — add specificity. Prompt: "Modern pop, 118 BPM, bright synth arpeggios, punchy drums, female vocal, soaring chorus with layered harmonies, Dua Lipa energy."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>4. R&B/Neo-Soul</strong> — BPM: 65-100. Instruments: Rhodes piano, smooth bass, finger snaps, lush pads, warm vocals. Mood: intimate, sensual, late-night. Prompt: "Neo-soul R&B, 88 BPM, warm Rhodes chords, smooth bass groove, subtle drum machine, intimate male vocal, candlelit atmosphere, SZA meets Frank Ocean."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>5. Electronic/House</strong> — BPM: 120-130. Instruments: four-on-the-floor kick, open hi-hats, synth stabs, bass drops, vocal chops. Mood: euphoric, dancefloor, energy. Prompt: "Deep house, 124 BPM, groovy bassline, shuffled hi-hats, atmospheric pads, vocal chop samples, 3 AM club energy, build to drop."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>6. Indie/Alternative</strong> — BPM: 100-140. Instruments: jangly electric guitars, analog synths, live-feeling drums, breathy vocals. Mood: wistful, authentic, emotional. AI sometimes struggles with the "imperfect" feel — add descriptors. Prompt: "Indie rock, 120 BPM, reverb-heavy jangly guitars, driving drums, breathy vocal, melancholic but hopeful, Radiohead meets Phoebe Bridgers."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>7. Country</strong> — BPM: 90-140. Instruments: acoustic guitar, pedal steel, fiddle, banjo, country twang vocal. Mood: storytelling, heartland, honest. Prompt: "Modern country, 105 BPM, acoustic guitar strumming, pedal steel slides, warm male vocal with slight twang, front-porch storytelling, summer night drive."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>8. Latin/Reggaeton</strong> — BPM: 85-100. Instruments: dembow rhythm, perreo bass, tropical percussion, brass hits, Latin guitar. Mood: party, sensual, summer. Prompt: "Reggaeton, 95 BPM, classic dembow rhythm, heavy bass, tropical percussion, Latin guitar riff, Bad Bunny energy, bilingual hook Spanish-English."</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Genre Blending</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The most interesting music lives between genres. AI makes blending effortless.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Genre blending is where AI producers have a genuine advantage over traditional producers. Blending two genres by hand requires deep knowledge of both. With AI, you just describe the fusion and let the model figure out the theory.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>The Blend Formula:</strong> "[Genre A] meets [Genre B], [tempo], [key instruments from A], [key instruments from B], [mood that bridges both]."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Blends that slap:</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">"Country trap" — acoustic guitar + 808s + twang vocal + trap hi-hats = Lil Nas X territory. "Lo-fi reggaeton" — dembow rhythm + vinyl texture + mellow synths = chill Latin vibes. "Jazz-tronica" — live jazz instruments + electronic production + house beat = Kaytranada zone. "Indie pop meets K-pop" — jangly guitars + K-pop song structure + bilingual lyrics = global crossover potential.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>Experiment wildly.</strong> AI doesn't judge your genre combinations. Some of the most viral tracks came from fusions nobody expected. Try "classical piano meets trap drums" or "bluegrass with electronic bass drops." The algorithm rewards novelty.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI Genre Strengths</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Play to the AI's strengths. Compensate for its weaknesses.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>AI excels at:</strong> Lo-fi (trained on tons of it), trap (repetitive 808 patterns are easy), pop (formulaic structure), electronic/EDM (synth-heavy = pure AI territory), cinematic/ambient (textures and atmospheres).</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>AI needs help with:</strong> Jazz (improvisation is inherently human), metal (complex guitar techniques), classical (emotional dynamics and rubato), live band feel (AI tends toward perfect quantization). For these genres, generate the base with AI and add human elements through editing or live recording on top.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>The human layer:</strong> After AI generates any genre, consider adding: slight tempo variations (humanize the drums), live instrument overdubs (even a simple acoustic guitar), vocal ad-libs and imperfections, and dynamic mixing (volume swells, filter automation). These touches transform "AI music" into "music made with AI."</p>
</div>
