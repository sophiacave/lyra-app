<!-- SECTION 1: WHY VISIBILITY MATTERS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Why This Matters</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your story has power. But telling it should not drain you.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Disabled people are underrepresented online. When you share your experience, you do two things: you help other disabled people feel less alone, and you educate non-disabled people about realities they do not see. That matters.</p>
  <p class="section-text">But content creation takes energy. Writing a post, editing it, finding the right tone, worrying about responses — it is exhausting. For people with limited spoons, the cost of advocacy can be too high.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>AI changes the equation.</strong> It drafts the post, refines the tone, handles the structure. You provide the experience and the truth. The energy cost drops from an hour to minutes.</p>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 3: BATCH CONTENT -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The System</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Create a week of content in one good hour.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">The smartest approach for variable-energy creators: batch your content during good periods. Create a week or month of posts when you have the energy, then schedule them to post automatically.</p>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Batch content prompt</div>

  [your condition]
- 2 educational posts about [disability topic]
- 1 resource share (recommend a tool, app, or
  community for disabled people)
- 1 light/funny post about disability life
- 1 engagement post (ask a question)

Platform: [Twitter/Instagram/LinkedIn]
My voice: [describe your tone — direct, warm, witty,
serious, etc.]
Topics I care about: [list 3-5]</code></pre>
</div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Schedule tools</div>
    <p>Use Buffer, Later, or the built-in scheduling in Twitter/Instagram to schedule posts ahead of time. Create during good days. Your online presence stays consistent even during bad periods.</p>
  </div>

<!-- SECTION 4: BOUNDARIES -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Protection</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Protecting yourself while being visible.</h2>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">You do not owe anyone your full story</div>
    <p>Share what you want to share. You can educate about disability without disclosing your specific diagnosis, medications, or worst moments. Ask AI: "Help me share [this experience] without disclosing [specific details I want to keep private]."</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Pre-written responses for trolls</div>
    <p>Ask AI to draft responses to common ableist comments. Save them as quick-reply templates. When someone is ignorant, paste the response instead of drafting one from scratch. Protect your energy.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">The mute/block strategy</div>
    <p>You are not obligated to engage with everyone. Mute and block liberally. Your mental health is more important than educating every stranger. Save your advocacy energy for people who are genuinely willing to learn.</p>
  </div>
</div>
