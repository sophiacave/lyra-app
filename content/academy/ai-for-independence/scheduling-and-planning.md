<!-- SECTION 1: EXECUTIVE FUNCTION -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Core Issue</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Executive function is not willpower. It is brain hardware.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Executive function is the brain's ability to plan, organize, prioritize, start tasks, and follow through. For people with ADHD, bipolar disorder, autism, traumatic brain injury, chronic fatigue, and many other conditions, executive function is unreliable. Not because of laziness — because of neurology.</p>
  <p class="section-text">When your executive function is impaired, even simple tasks feel impossibly complex. "Schedule a dentist appointment" becomes: find the number, call (anxiety), navigate the phone tree, pick a time, remember to write it down, remember you wrote it down, actually go. Each step requires executive function you may not have.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>AI becomes your external executive function.</strong> It breaks tasks into steps, reminds you what to do next, and handles the logistics you cannot face.</p>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 3: ENERGY-AWARE ROUTINES -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Routines</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Build routines that flex with your energy.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Rigid routines fail for people with variable energy. You need routines that adapt to how you feel today — not how you felt yesterday or how you hope to feel tomorrow.</p>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Energy-aware routine prompt</div>
<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>Create a daily routine with 3 versions:

LOW ENERGY DAY: Only absolute essentials. Meds,
water, one meal, one basic hygiene task. Nothing
else required. Everything else is bonus.

MEDIUM ENERGY DAY: Essentials plus 2-3 productive
tasks. Email triage, one appointment, light chores.
Include rest breaks between tasks.

HIGH ENERGY DAY: Full productive day. All tasks from
medium plus bigger projects, errands, social
interaction. But still include rest periods.

Each version should feel achievable, not aspirational.
On a low day, completing the low list IS a full day.</code></pre>
</div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 4: APPOINTMENT MANAGEMENT -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Appointments</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Never miss an appointment again.</h2>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">AI appointment script</div>
    <p>Dreading a phone call? Ask AI to write you a script: "Write a phone script for calling [office] to [schedule/cancel/reschedule] an appointment. Include what to say when they answer, what information they will probably ask for, and how to handle being put on hold." Read the script. Make the call. Cross it off.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Online booking first</div>
    <p>Before calling, ask AI: "Does [doctor/office name] have online appointment booking?" Many offices now let you book online, skipping the phone call entirely. AI can help you find the booking link.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Reminder system</div>
    <p>Ask AI to create a reminder schedule: "Set up reminders for my appointment on [date]: 1 week before, 1 day before, and 2 hours before. Include the address and what I need to bring." Put these in your calendar app.</p>
  </div>
</div>
