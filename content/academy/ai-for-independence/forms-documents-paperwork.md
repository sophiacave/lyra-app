<!-- SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Problem</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Paperwork is a disability tax.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Disabled people deal with more paperwork than almost anyone. Insurance forms. Disability applications. Medical records requests. Accommodation letters. Appeals. Legal documents. Tax forms with special deductions. <strong>The people with the least energy face the most bureaucracy.</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">Each form takes focus, energy, and often physical effort to fill out. When you have limited spoons, a stack of paperwork can feel like a brick wall between you and the help you need.</p>
  <p class="section-text">AI changes this. Not by removing the paperwork, but by doing 90% of the work for you.</p>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 2: FILLING FORMS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Forms</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Let AI fill the form. You just check the answers.</h2>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Form-filling prompt — copy and customize</div>
<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>I need to fill out a [type of form]. Here is my
information:

Name: [your name]
Address: [your address]
DOB: [your date of birth]
SSN: [last 4 only if needed]
Condition: [your disability/condition]
Doctor: [doctor name and practice]

The form asks about [describe what the form wants].

Fill out every field based on my information. For
questions I have not answered, mark them [NEED INFO]
so I know what to fill in myself.</code></pre>
</div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Save a personal info template</div>
    <p>Create a text file with your standard information — name, address, phone, doctor's info, medications, diagnosis dates. Keep it somewhere secure. When you need to fill a form, paste it into Claude along with the form questions. You type once, AI fills forms forever.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 5: PRIVACY -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Important</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Privacy: what to share and what to protect.</h2>

  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>Be careful with sensitive information.</strong> Do not paste your full Social Security number, bank account numbers, or passwords into AI. Use partial information (last 4 of SSN) and fill in sensitive fields yourself. AI conversations are generally private, but there is no reason to share more than necessary.</p>
  </div>
</div>

  <p><strong>The key insight:</strong> Paperwork should not be the barrier between you and the help you need. <strong>AI turns hours of form-filling into minutes of reviewing.</strong> Save your energy for the things that matter. Let AI handle the bureaucracy.</p>
