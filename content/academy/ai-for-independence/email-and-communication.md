<!-- SECTION 1: THE ENERGY COST OF EMAIL -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Problem</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">An inbox is an energy vampire.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">For people with limited energy, a full inbox is not just annoying — it is debilitating. Each email requires reading, processing, deciding, and responding. When you have 20 unread messages, that is 20 decision points draining your limited battery.</p>
  <p class="section-text">When you are in a depressive episode, a flare-up, or a bad pain day, emails pile up. Then the pile creates anxiety about the pile. Then the anxiety makes it even harder to deal with the pile. <strong>AI breaks this cycle.</strong></p>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 3: INBOX TRIAGE -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Triage</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Sort 50 emails in 5 minutes.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">When your inbox is overwhelming, do not read every email. Let AI triage for you.</p>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Inbox triage prompt</div>
<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>Here are my unread emails (subject lines and senders):

[paste the list]

Sort them into 3 categories:
1. URGENT — needs response today
2. THIS WEEK — important but not urgent
3. IGNORE — newsletters, promotions, FYI-only

For each URGENT email, draft a brief response I can
send immediately.</code></pre>
</div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Low-energy inbox strategy</div>
    <p>On bad days, only deal with URGENT emails. On medium days, handle URGENT and THIS WEEK. On good days, clear the backlog. AI does the sorting so you spend your energy only on what matters today.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 4: PROFESSIONAL PRESENCE -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Staying Visible</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Maintain professional presence even during bad periods.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">One of the cruelest aspects of episodic disabilities is that the world does not pause when you do. Clients expect responses. Employers expect participation. But you can build systems that maintain your presence even during bad periods.</p>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Auto-responder templates</div>
    <p>Ask AI to write professional auto-responders: "I am currently dealing with a health matter and may be slower to respond. For urgent items, please [alternative contact]. I will respond to all messages by [date]." Set it up during a good period so it is ready when you need it.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Batch processing</div>
    <p>Instead of checking email throughout the day (energy death by a thousand cuts), batch process once or twice a day. Open inbox, paste subject lines to AI for triage, respond to urgent items, close inbox. Total time: 15 minutes instead of constant anxiety.</p>
  </div>
</div>
