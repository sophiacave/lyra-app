<!-- SECTION 1: THE REALITY -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Reality</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Disability is expensive. Ignoring money makes it worse.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Disabled people face higher costs (medications, equipment, transportation, copays) and often lower income (reduced work hours, gaps in employment, discrimination). This financial pressure means every dollar matters — but executive dysfunction, brain fog, and overwhelm make money management feel impossible.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The result: late fees on bills you forgot. Overdraft charges because you lost track. Medical debt because you did not appeal an insurance denial in time. Not because you are irresponsible. <strong>Because the system demands executive function you do not always have.</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">AI cannot make you richer. But it can make sure you never miss a bill, never pay a fee you could avoid, and always know where you stand financially — with minimal energy spent.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Bills</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Never miss a bill again.</h2>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Bill tracker setup prompt</div>
<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>Create a monthly bill tracker for me. Here are my
recurring bills:

[List your bills: name, amount, due date]
Example:
- Rent: $1,200, due 1st
- Electric: ~$80, due 15th
- Phone: $45, due 20th
- Insurance: $350, due 1st

For each bill:
1. Which ones can be set to autopay?
2. Which ones need manual payment?
3. Create a calendar reminder schedule (3 days before
   each due date)
4. Calculate my total monthly fixed costs
5. Flag which bills have late fees and how much</code></pre>
</div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Autopay everything possible</div>
    <p>The single best financial move for people with executive dysfunction: set every bill to autopay. Ask AI: "Which of my bills can I set to autopay, and what are the steps for each one?" Autopay means the bill gets paid even when you cannot function. It is insurance against bad episodes.</p>
  </div>

<!-- SECTION 4: DECODING BILLS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Decoding</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Understand what you are actually being charged.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Medical bills, insurance Explanation of Benefits, utility bills with mysterious charges — paste them into AI and ask for a plain English translation.</p>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">

<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>Explain this bill/statement in plain English:

[paste the bill details]

Tell me:
1. What am I actually being charged for?
2. Does this amount seem right?
3. Is anything on here that I should question or
   dispute?
4. Is there a payment plan option I should ask about?</code></pre>
</div>

<div class="callout purple">
  <p><strong>The key insight:</strong> Financial management for disabled people is not about discipline — it is about <strong>systems.</strong> Autopay handles the bills. AI handles the math. A 10-minute monthly check-in handles the awareness. Build the system once. It runs even when you cannot.</p>
