<!-- SECTION 1: THE HEALTHCARE GAUNTLET -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Reality</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The healthcare system was not built for disabled people.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Short appointments where you forget to mention important symptoms. Insurance denials written in legal language. Medical records you cannot understand. Phone trees that take 40 minutes to navigate. <strong>The system requires advocacy skills and energy at the exact moments when you have the least of both.</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">AI does not replace a good doctor or a good therapist. But it gives you the preparation, documentation, and language to get the most out of every medical interaction — even when your brain is not cooperating.</p>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 2: APPOINTMENT PREP -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Appointments</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Walk into every appointment prepared.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">The number one regret after a doctor appointment: "I forgot to mention..." AI prevents this.</p>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Appointment prep prompt</div>
<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>I have a [type] appointment with Dr. [name] on
[date]. Help me prepare:

My conditions: [list]
Current medications: [list]
New symptoms since last visit: [describe]
Questions I want to ask: [list any you have]

Create:
1. A one-page summary I can hand the doctor
2. A list of questions I should ask (including any I
   might not think of)
3. A list of symptoms organized by severity
4. Reminder: what to bring (insurance card, med list,
   etc.)

Keep the summary concise — doctors have limited time.</code></pre>
</div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Print it or keep it on your phone</div>
    <p>Print the AI-generated summary or keep it open on your phone. When brain fog hits mid-appointment, you read from the list instead of trying to remember. <strong>This one strategy changes the quality of every medical visit.</strong></p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 4: INSURANCE APPEALS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Insurance Fights</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Fight insurance denials without burning out.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Insurance companies deny claims expecting most people to give up. <strong>Do not give up. Let AI fight for you.</strong></p>

<div style="background:#0a0a0a;border:1px solid rgba(255,255,255,.06);border-radius:10px;padding:1.25rem;margin:1rem 0;font-family:'JetBrains Mono',monospace;font-size:.82rem;color:#a1a1aa;line-height:1.7;overflow-x:auto">
<div style="font-size:.7rem;color:#71717a;margin-bottom:.5rem;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:.05em">Insurance appeal prompt</div>
<pre style="margin:0;color:#e5e5e5"><code>My insurance denied [treatment/medication/procedure].
Their reason: [paste the denial reason].
My condition: [your diagnosis].
My doctor recommended this because: [reason].

Write a formal appeal letter that:
1. References the specific denial and claim number
2. Explains medical necessity
3. Cites relevant medical guidelines if applicable
4. Requests a peer-to-peer review
5. Notes the appeal deadline
6. Is professional but firm

Address it to [insurance company appeals department].</code></pre>
</div>

  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>Important:</strong> AI-drafted appeal letters are a starting point. Have your doctor review and sign off on any medical claims in the letter. The combination of AI-drafted language and your doctor's authority is powerful.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 5: RESEARCHING CONDITIONS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Research</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Research without falling into misinformation.</h2>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">The research prompt that avoids misinformation</div>
    <p>"Explain [condition/treatment] based on current medical consensus. Cite major medical organizations (NIH, WHO, Mayo Clinic). What does the evidence actually show? What is still uncertain? What questions should I ask my doctor?" AI sticks to evidence-based information when you tell it to.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Preparing questions for your doctor</div>
    <p>"I have [condition]. I read about [treatment/approach]. Help me create a list of informed questions to ask my doctor about whether this could be appropriate for me. I want to have a productive conversation, not demand a specific treatment."</p>
  </div>
</div>
