<!-- SECTION 1: YOUR DISABILITY, YOUR TOOLKIT -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Personalization</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">No two disabilities are the same. No two toolkits should be either.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">A toolkit for someone with chronic fatigue looks different from one for someone with visual impairment, which looks different from one for someone with ADHD. The best toolkit is the one built around <strong>your specific barriers.</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">Start by listing the tasks that cost you the most energy, cause the most pain, or get dropped most often. Those are your priorities. Everything else is secondary.</p>

<!-- SECTION 4: SOVEREIGNTY -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Sovereignty</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Own your tools. Do not rent your independence.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Here is a hard truth: if your entire system depends on one company's product, and that company changes its pricing, terms, or shuts down — you lose your infrastructure. For disabled people, losing infrastructure is not just inconvenient. It can be dangerous.</p>

  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>The sovereign approach:</strong> Use multiple tools so no single point of failure exists. Keep your personal data (info template, med list, contacts) in local files you control, not just in cloud apps. Export your data regularly. Have a backup for every critical system. Your independence should not depend on any single company's business decisions.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Practical sovereignty checklist</div>
    <p>Can you switch AI providers and still function? Is your personal info saved locally, not just in the cloud? Do you have backup reminders if your phone dies? Can someone else access your critical info in an emergency? If any answer is no, that is your next thing to fix.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- KEY TAKEAWAY -->
<div class="callout purple">
  <p><strong>The key insight:</strong> Your toolkit should be built around your barriers, not around what is trendy. Choose tools that address <strong>your specific challenges.</strong> Build workflows that run even on your worst days. Own your infrastructure. Your independence depends on it.</p>
