<!-- SECTION 2: MAINTENANCE -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Maintenance</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Keeping your systems running with minimal effort.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">A system that requires constant maintenance is a system that will fail during bad periods. Your infrastructure should be <strong>low-maintenance by design.</strong></p>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Monthly system check (15 minutes)</div>
    <p>Once a month, on a good day, run through: Are my autopays still active? Is my personal info template current? Are my calendar reminders working? Do I have upcoming appointments that need prep? Any tools that changed or need updating? Set a recurring calendar reminder.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">The good-day investment</div>
    <p>On good days, invest 30 minutes in improving your systems. Update a template. Pre-write crisis communications. Create a new workflow. Batch social media content. These investments pay dividends on bad days. Think of good-day energy as deposits into a system that pays interest during bad periods.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Emergency mode</div>
    <p>Build an emergency mode checklist: the absolute minimum to keep your life running during a crisis. Meds taken. One meal eaten. Urgent emails answered (using templates). Everything else can wait. This is the low-energy day routine from Lesson 5, but for your entire system.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 3: AI AS ACCOMMODATIONS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Advocacy</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">AI as a reasonable accommodation.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Under the ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) and similar laws worldwide, you have the right to reasonable accommodations in employment, education, and public services. AI tools can be framed as assistive technology — because that is exactly what they are.</p>
