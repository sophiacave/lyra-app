<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Shift</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Resumes are dead. Reputation is everything.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Nobody reads resumes anymore. Hiring managers Google you. Clients check your LinkedIn. Collaborators look at your portfolio. Your personal brand is what they find -- or don't find.</p>
    <p>Here's the uncomfortable truth: if you don't control your narrative, someone else will. Or worse, there's nothing to find at all. In 2026, invisibility is the real career risk.</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>The compound effect:</strong> Every piece of content you publish, every connection you make, every project you share -- it compounds over time. A year of consistent personal branding creates opportunities that cold-applying to 500 jobs never will.
    </div>
    <p>This isn't about becoming an influencer. It's about being findable, credible, and memorable in your field.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI Advantage</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Building a brand used to take a team. Now it takes you.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Five years ago, building a personal brand meant hiring a designer for your website, a copywriter for your bio, a social media manager for your posts, and a photographer for your headshots. Thousands of dollars before you published anything.</p>
    <p>Now? AI handles all of it. You focus on being interesting -- AI handles the production.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>What AI does for your brand:</strong> Writes your bio, designs your website, creates social posts, generates content ideas, drafts newsletters, edits your writing, and maintains consistency across platforms. You provide the expertise and personality. AI does the rest.
    </div>
    <p>The playing field is level. A college student with AI tools can build a brand that looks as polished as a Fortune 500 executive's. The only differentiator is whether you actually do it.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Three Pillars</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The foundation of every strong brand.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Every memorable personal brand stands on three pillars:</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>1. Clarity:</strong> What do you do? Who do you help? What's your unique angle? If someone reads your profile and can't answer these in 5 seconds, your brand isn't clear enough.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>2. Consistency:</strong> Same voice, same visual style, same messaging across every platform. Your LinkedIn, portfolio, Twitter, and newsletter should feel like they come from the same person.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>3. Credibility:</strong> Proof that you know what you're talking about. Case studies, projects, testimonials, content that demonstrates expertise. Talk is cheap -- work speaks.
    </div>
    <p>This course builds all three, lesson by lesson. By lesson 10, you'll have a complete personal brand machine powered by AI.</p>
  </div>
</div>
