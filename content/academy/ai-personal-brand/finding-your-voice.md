<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Discovery</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">You already have a voice. Let's find it.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Your brand voice isn't something you invent from scratch. It's already in you -- in how you explain things to friends, how you text, how you talk about your work when you're excited about it.</p>
    <p>Here's the exercise: write a message to a friend explaining what you do and why you love it. Don't think about it. Just write it like you'd text someone. That raw, unfiltered version? That's closer to your brand voice than any polished bio.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>Voice audit prompt:</strong> Give AI 3-5 things you've written (emails, social posts, messages) and ask: "Analyze my writing style. What words do I overuse? What's my sentence length? Am I formal or casual? Do I use humor? What patterns do you see?" The results are revealing.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Components</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Voice vs. tone vs. personality.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>These three get confused constantly. Let's untangle them:</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Voice:</strong> Your core identity. It doesn't change. Are you bold or thoughtful? Technical or accessible? Serious or playful? Your voice is consistent across everything you publish.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Tone:</strong> How your voice adapts to context. You're still you, but your tone shifts. A LinkedIn post is different from a tweet is different from a keynote. Same voice, different tone.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Personality:</strong> The human qualities that make you memorable. Quirks, opinions, recurring themes, the things only YOU would say. This is what AI can't generate without your input.
    </div>
    <p>Think of it like music: voice is the instrument, tone is the genre you're playing, personality is your signature riff.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Training AI</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Make AI sound like you, not like a robot.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>The biggest mistake: using AI to write and publishing the output directly. AI-generated content without your voice sounds like everyone else's AI-generated content. Generic. Forgettable.</p>
    <p>The fix: create a voice prompt that you paste before every AI writing request.</p>
    <div class="demo-container">
      <p><em>"Write in my brand voice. I'm [casual/formal]. I use [short/long] sentences. My tone is [energetic/thoughtful/witty]. I [do/don't] use jargon. I often reference [topics/themes]. I never say [words/phrases you hate]. My audience is [who]. Examples of my writing: [paste 2-3 samples]."</em></p>
    </div>
    <p>Save this prompt. Use it every single time. Over time, refine it as you learn what works and what doesn't. This is your brand voice API -- the interface between your personality and AI's output.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Brand Voice Guide</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Document it once, use it forever.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Create a simple one-page document with:</p>
    <p><strong>Voice keywords:</strong> 3-5 adjectives that describe your voice (e.g., "bold, practical, witty, accessible, opinionated").</p>
    <p><strong>Do/Don't list:</strong> "I DO use metaphors and real examples. I DON'T use corporate jargon or passive voice."</p>
    <p><strong>Sample sentences:</strong> 3-5 sentences that perfectly capture your voice. These become reference points for AI and for yourself.</p>
    <p><strong>Audience definition:</strong> Who you're talking to. Their level of expertise, what they care about, what language they use.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>Living document:</strong> Your voice evolves. Update this guide every quarter. What felt right six months ago might feel stale now. Growth is good -- let your brand voice grow with you.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
