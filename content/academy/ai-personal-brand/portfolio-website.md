<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Why a Website</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Social profiles are rented land. Your website is owned.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>LinkedIn can change their algorithm. Twitter can ban your account. Instagram can disappear. Your website? That's yours forever. It's the one place on the internet you fully control.</p>
    <p>Every link in your bio, every email signature, every business card -- they all point back to your website. It's the hub of your entire brand.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>The 5-second rule:</strong> A visitor decides whether to stay or leave within 5 seconds. Your website needs to answer three questions instantly: Who are you? What do you do? Why should I care?
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Essential Pages</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Four pages. That's all you need to start.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Don't overthink it. A portfolio website needs four pages:</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Home:</strong> Your hook. A clear headline, a one-sentence description of what you do, and a call to action. This is your elevator pitch in web form.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>About:</strong> Your story. Not your resume -- your story. Why you do what you do. What drives you. Include a professional photo (AI can help you edit one).
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Work/Projects:</strong> Proof of expertise. 3-5 of your best projects with descriptions, images, and results. Quality over quantity always.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Contact:</strong> How to reach you. Email, social links, and optionally a contact form. Make it ridiculously easy for opportunities to find you.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Building It</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">From zero to live in one afternoon.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Platform options ranked by simplicity:</p>
    <p><strong>Framer:</strong> AI-powered website builder. Describe your site and it generates a design. Drag-and-drop editing. Custom domains. Free tier available.</p>
    <p><strong>Webflow:</strong> More control, more complexity. Great for design-heavy portfolios. Learning curve is steeper but output is stunning.</p>
    <p><strong>Next.js + Vercel:</strong> If you code (or want AI to code for you). Maximum flexibility. Free hosting. Ask AI to generate a complete portfolio site in Next.js.</p>
    <div class="demo-container">
      <p><em>"Build me a personal portfolio website. I'm a [your role]. Include: hero section with my name and tagline, about section with my story, project grid showing 4 projects with images and descriptions, contact section with email and social links. Use a clean, minimal design with lots of white space. Dark mode support."</em></p>
    </div>
    <p>Whichever platform you choose, get version 1 live today. You can always improve it later. A live imperfect site beats a perfect site that exists only in your head.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">SEO</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Be findable when people search your name.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Search Engine Optimization sounds scary but the basics are simple. You want your website to be the first result when someone Googles your name.</p>
    <p><strong>Title tag:</strong> "[Your Name] - [What You Do]" on every page. This is what shows up in Google results.</p>
    <p><strong>Meta description:</strong> A 150-character summary of each page. Write it like a hook -- make people want to click.</p>
    <p><strong>Headings:</strong> Use H1 for your name, H2 for sections. Google reads these to understand your page structure.</p>
    <p><strong>Speed:</strong> Compress images, minimize code. A slow site ranks lower and loses visitors. Most website builders handle this automatically.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>AI SEO prompt:</strong> "Write SEO-optimized title tags and meta descriptions for my portfolio website. My name is [name], I'm a [role], and I specialize in [specialty]. Make each description compelling and under 155 characters."
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
