<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Profile Optimization</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your profile is a landing page, not a resume.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Most LinkedIn profiles read like a boring resume. "Experienced professional with 10+ years in..." Nobody cares. Your profile should read like a landing page that makes people want to connect.</p>
    <p><strong>Headline:</strong> Not your job title. Your value proposition. "I help startups ship AI products 3x faster" beats "Senior Software Engineer at Company X."</p>
    <p><strong>About section:</strong> Write in first person. Tell your story. What problem do you solve? What's your unique perspective? End with a clear call to action.</p>
    <p><strong>Banner image:</strong> Use Canva or AI to create a custom banner. Include your name, what you do, and your website URL. Free real estate -- don't leave it blank.</p>
    <div class="demo-container">
      <p><em>"Write a LinkedIn headline and About section for me. I'm a [role] who specializes in [area]. My unique angle is [what makes you different]. I want to attract [clients/employers/collaborators]. Write in first person, conversational tone, and end with a call to action to visit my website."</em></p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Content That Works</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">What actually gets engagement on LinkedIn.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>LinkedIn's algorithm rewards content that keeps people on the platform. Here's what works in 2026:</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Personal stories with professional lessons:</strong> "I got rejected from 47 jobs before landing at [Company]. Here's what I learned..." These go viral because they're relatable and vulnerable.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Contrarian takes:</strong> Challenge conventional wisdom in your industry. "Unpopular opinion: [common practice] is actually hurting your [outcome]." Disagreement drives comments, comments drive reach.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>How-to breakdowns:</strong> Step-by-step posts that teach something useful. "5 ways I use AI to [do something your audience cares about]." Practical value gets saved and shared.
    </div>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>Formatting matters:</strong> Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences). Line breaks between every thought. Hook in the first line. LinkedIn is mobile-first -- walls of text get scrolled past.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Networking</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Connect like a human, not a bot.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Networking on LinkedIn doesn't mean blasting connection requests to strangers. It means building genuine relationships at scale.</p>
    <p><strong>The engagement-first strategy:</strong> Before connecting with someone, engage with their content for 2 weeks. Comment thoughtfully on 3-5 of their posts. When you send the connection request, they already recognize your name.</p>
    <p><strong>Connection messages:</strong> Keep them short and specific. "Hey [Name], loved your post about [topic]. I'm working on something similar and would love to connect." Never pitch in the connection request. Ever.</p>
    <p><strong>DMs that work:</strong> Give before you ask. Share something relevant to them (an article, a tool, an intro) before you ever ask for anything. Generosity builds real networks.</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>The 5-5-5 daily routine:</strong> Spend 15 minutes: comment on 5 posts in your industry, reply to 5 comments on your own posts, send 5 thoughtful connection requests. Consistency compounds into a powerful network.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI Workflow</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Maintain presence without living on the platform.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>You don't need to spend hours on LinkedIn. Here's the AI-powered weekly workflow:</p>
    <p><strong>Sunday (30 min):</strong> Use AI to draft 3-5 posts for the week. Edit with your voice. Schedule with Buffer or LinkedIn's native scheduler.</p>
    <p><strong>Daily (10 min):</strong> Check notifications. Reply to comments on your posts. Leave 3-5 thoughtful comments on others' posts. That's it.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>Comment quality:</strong> "Great post!" is useless. Add value in your comments. Share a related experience, ask a thoughtful question, or expand on a point. High-quality comments get you noticed by the original poster AND their audience.
    </div>
    <p>AI can draft your comments too, but always personalize them. A comment that's obviously AI-generated does more harm than no comment at all.</p>
  </div>
</div>
