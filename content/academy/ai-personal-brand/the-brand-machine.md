<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The System</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">A brand is not a task. It's a machine.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">You have learned how to find your voice, build a portfolio, create content, grow on LinkedIn, speak publicly, write newsletters, establish thought leadership, and monetize your expertise. Nine powerful skills. But skills without a system are just potential energy.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The Brand Machine turns all of that into a self-sustaining loop. Content feeds your newsletter. Your newsletter feeds LinkedIn. LinkedIn feeds speaking opportunities. Speaking feeds thought leadership. Thought leadership feeds clients. Clients feed case studies. Case studies feed content. The loop never stops.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>The compound effect:</strong> A single blog post might get 50 views. But repurposed into a LinkedIn post, a newsletter segment, three tweets, and a podcast talking point — that same idea reaches 5,000 people across five platforms. AI handles the repurposing. You handle the ideas.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Metrics That Matter</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Track three numbers. Ignore everything else.</h2>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">1. Engagement Rate</div>
    <p>Comments + saves + shares divided by impressions. Above 3% means your content resonates. Below 1% means you are broadcasting, not connecting.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">2. Email List Growth</div>
    <p>New subscribers per week. This is the only platform you truly own. Social algorithms change. Your email list is forever. Target 20-50 new subscribers per week.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">3. Revenue Per Subscriber</div>
    <p>Total revenue divided by email list size. A healthy personal brand generates $1-5 per subscriber per month. 1,000 subscribers at $0 revenue means your monetization is broken.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The 90-Day Plan</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your brand launch in three phases.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 1-30: Foundation.</strong> Set up your portfolio site, define your voice, publish 12 pieces of content across 2-3 platforms. Build your first lead magnet and start collecting emails. Goal: 100 email subscribers.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 31-60: Momentum.</strong> Launch your newsletter, apply to 3 speaking opportunities, and publish your first thought leadership piece. Start engaging deeply in your niche community. Goal: 500 email subscribers and your first inbound lead.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 61-90: Monetization.</strong> Launch your first paid offering — consulting, course, or digital product. Refine your content based on analytics. Double down on what works. Goal: first $1,000 from your personal brand.</p>
  <div class="callout purple">
    <p><strong>The key insight from this entire course:</strong> Your personal brand is not about being famous. It is about being <strong>findable</strong> by the right people at the right time. AI removes every excuse — it handles the writing, the scheduling, the repurposing, and the analytics. All you need to bring is your expertise and your willingness to share it. Start Monday.</p>
  </div>
