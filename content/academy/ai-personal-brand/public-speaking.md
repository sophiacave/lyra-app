<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Why Speaking</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">One talk does more than 100 posts.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Public speaking is the ultimate brand accelerator. A single 20-minute talk puts you in front of a room full of potential clients, collaborators, and fans. And when that talk gets recorded? It lives forever.</p>
    <p>Here's what most people get wrong: they think you need to be invited to speak. You don't. Meetups, podcasts, webinars, Twitter Spaces, LinkedIn Live -- there are hundreds of stages waiting for someone with something useful to say.</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>The authority shortcut:</strong> When you're the person on stage, the audience automatically assumes you're an expert. Speaking is the fastest way to build credibility in any field. One conference talk can generate more inbound leads than a year of posting.
    </div>
    <p>And here's the secret -- AI makes the hard parts easy. Writing the talk, building the slides, preparing your talking points. The only thing you bring is your presence and your perspective.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Talk Structure</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Write a talk that keeps the room awake.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Every great talk follows a simple structure: Hook, Problem, Framework, Proof, Action. AI can draft the whole thing once you feed it the right inputs.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>The 5-part talk formula:</strong> (1) Hook -- a surprising stat, story, or question in the first 30 seconds. (2) Problem -- the pain your audience feels. (3) Framework -- your unique solution or mental model. (4) Proof -- case studies, results, demos. (5) Action -- one clear thing they should do next.
    </div>
    <div class="demo-container">
      <p><em>"Write a 15-minute talk outline for [your niche] audience. Topic: [subject]. Use this structure: open with a surprising hook, define the problem, present a 3-step framework, include two real-world examples, and end with one clear call to action. Tone: conversational, not academic."</em></p>
    </div>
    <p>AI gives you the skeleton. You add the stories only you can tell -- the failures, the weird experiments, the moments that shaped your perspective. That's what makes a talk memorable.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Slides & Visuals</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Slides that support you, not replace you.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Nobody wants to watch you read bullet points off a screen. The best slides are visual anchors -- one image, one phrase, one data point per slide. Let your voice carry the content.</p>
    <p><strong>Tools that make it fast:</strong> Gamma.app generates full decks from a text prompt. Beautiful.ai auto-formats your content into clean layouts. Canva's AI features handle custom graphics. You can go from outline to finished deck in under an hour.</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>The 10-20-30 rule:</strong> 10 slides max, 20 minutes max, 30-point minimum font size. If you need more slides than that, your talk isn't focused enough. Constraints force clarity.
    </div>
    <p>Record yourself rehearsing with your slides. Watch it back. If any slide makes you pause awkwardly or read from the screen, cut it or simplify it. Your slides should feel like a conversation partner, not a teleprompter.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Landing Stages</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Find stages before you feel ready.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>You don't wait until you're "ready" to start speaking. You get ready by speaking. Start small and build up:</p>
    <p><strong>Level 1:</strong> Local meetups, company lunch-and-learns, Twitter Spaces. Low stakes, high learning.</p>
    <p><strong>Level 2:</strong> Podcast guest appearances, webinars, LinkedIn Live sessions. Wider reach, still comfortable.</p>
    <p><strong>Level 3:</strong> Conference talks, panels, keynotes. Big stage energy. You'll be ready because you did Levels 1 and 2 first.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>The pitch template:</strong> "Hi [Organizer], I'm [Name], a [role] who specializes in [topic]. I'd love to present [specific talk title] to your audience. It covers [key takeaway] and I've [credibility proof]. Here's a 2-minute video of me speaking: [link]." Keep it short. Make it easy to say yes.
    </div>
    <p>Use AI to find conferences in your niche, draft pitch emails, and customize your talk abstract for each event. The hardest part is hitting send -- AI handles the rest.</p>
  </div>
</div>
