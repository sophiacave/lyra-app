<!-- SECTION 1: THE SHIFT -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Big Idea</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">You describe it. AI builds it.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">For decades, building software meant learning a programming language, memorizing syntax, debugging cryptic error messages, and spending years mastering a craft before you could build anything useful. That wall kept millions of people with great ideas on the outside looking in.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Vibe coding tears that wall down.</strong> Instead of writing code line by line, you describe what you want in plain English. "Build me a to-do app with categories and a dark mode." The AI writes the code. You review the result. You refine with more conversation. The app gets built.</p>
  <p class="section-text">This is not a gimmick or a toy. People are shipping real products, launching real businesses, and automating real workflows using nothing but conversation with AI coding tools. The barrier to entry for software just dropped from "years of study" to "can you describe what you want?"</p>

  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>Real talk:</strong> Vibe coding does not mean code quality does not matter. It means <strong>you do not have to be the one writing every line.</strong> You become the architect, the designer, the decision-maker. AI becomes the builder who executes your vision.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 2: ANALOGY -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Think Of It Like This</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">You are the director. AI is the film crew.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Imagine you want to make a movie. In the old world, you would need to learn how to operate a camera, set up lighting, edit footage, mix sound, and build sets. That is traditional coding — doing everything yourself.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Vibe coding is like being a film director. You say "I want a scene with warm lighting, two characters sitting in a cafe, tension in the dialogue." The crew makes it happen. You review the take, give notes, and they adjust. <strong>Your job is the vision. Their job is the execution.</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">The director who cannot operate a boom mic is not less valuable than the sound engineer. They are doing a different job — and it is the job that determines what actually gets built.</p>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 4: WHO IS THIS FOR -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Is This For You?</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Vibe coding is for anyone with an idea and a keyboard.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">You do not need a computer science degree. You do not need to know what JavaScript is. You do not need to understand databases, APIs, or deployment pipelines. If you can describe what you want clearly, you can vibe code.</p>
  <p class="section-text">That said, vibe coding works best when you think clearly about <strong>what</strong> you want to build. The AI handles the <strong>how</strong>. Your job is the <strong>what</strong> and the <strong>why</strong>. That is what this course teaches you.</p>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Who thrives at vibe coding</div>
    <p>People who can explain what they want. People who notice when something does not look right. People who iterate — try something, see the result, adjust. If you have ever given clear instructions to another human, you already have the core skill.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 5: COMMON CONCERNS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Honest Answers</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">But is it "real" coding?</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Yes. The code AI generates is real code. It runs in real browsers, on real servers, for real users. Whether a human typed it character by character or an AI generated it from a conversation does not change what the code does.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Think about it this way: nobody asks if a building is a "real" building because the architect used CAD software instead of hand-drawing blueprints. The tool changed. The result is the same.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Will you write code as efficient as a senior engineer with 15 years of experience? Probably not at first. But you will build things that <strong>work</strong>, that <strong>solve problems</strong>, and that <strong>you control</strong>. And as you learn, your vibe-coded projects will get better and better.</p>
</div>

<!-- KEY TAKEAWAY -->
<div class="callout purple">
  <p><strong>The key insight:</strong> Vibe coding is not about replacing programmers. It is about <strong>unlocking the millions of people who have ideas but were locked out of building them.</strong> If you can describe what you want, you can build software. That is the revolution.</p>
