<!-- SECTION 3: SETTING UP -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Get Started</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Setting up in under 5 minutes.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Each tool has a different setup process. Here is the fastest path to getting started with each one.</p>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Replit (30 seconds)</div>
    <p>Go to <strong>replit.com</strong>. Create a free account. Click "Create Repl." Choose "Agent" mode. Describe your app in the text box. That is literally it. Your app starts building immediately.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Cursor (3 minutes)</div>
    <p>Go to <strong>cursor.com</strong>. Download the app for your computer. Open it. Create a new project folder. Press <strong>Cmd+K</strong> (Mac) or <strong>Ctrl+K</strong> (Windows) to open the AI prompt. Describe what you want. Cursor starts writing code in the editor.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">Claude Code (5 minutes)</div>
    <p>Open your terminal. Run <strong>npm install -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code</strong>. Navigate to your project folder. Type <strong>claude</strong>. Start describing what you want to build. Claude reads your project and starts creating files.</p>
  </div>
</div>
