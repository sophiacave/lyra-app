<!-- SECTION 3: COMMON PATTERNS -->
<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Quick Wins</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">5 design patterns that make anything look professional.</h2>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">1. Consistent spacing</div>
    <p>Tell AI: "Use consistent spacing throughout. Same padding in all cards, same gap between sections." Inconsistent spacing is the #1 thing that makes an app look amateur.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">2. Maximum width on content</div>
    <p>Tell AI: "Content should not stretch wider than 800px on desktop. Center it on the page." Text that stretches across the entire screen is hard to read and looks cheap.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">3. Hover states on interactive elements</div>
    <p>Tell AI: "Add hover effects to all buttons and clickable cards. Subtle color change and maybe a slight lift/shadow." This makes apps feel responsive and alive.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">4. One accent color only</div>
    <p>Tell AI: "Use one accent color for all interactive elements — buttons, links, active states. Everything else should be neutral (grays, whites, blacks)." One color = clean. Five colors = chaos.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="tip-box">
    <div class="tip-label">5. Mobile-first</div>
    <p>Tell AI: "Make sure this looks good on mobile. Stack elements vertically on small screens. Make touch targets at least 44px." More than half your users will see your app on a phone first.</p>
  </div>
</div>
