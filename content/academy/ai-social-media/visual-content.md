<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Design Basics</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">You don't need a degree. You need 4 principles.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Contrast:</strong> Make the important thing pop. Dark text on light backgrounds (or vice versa). If everything is the same size and color, nothing stands out. Your eye should immediately know where to look. High contrast between your subject and background is why professional content looks professional.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Hierarchy:</strong> Guide the viewer's eye in order. The most important element should be the largest and most prominent. Supporting elements should be smaller. On a carousel slide, that means: headline (biggest), supporting text (medium), source or caption (smallest). Three levels maximum.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>White space:</strong> Empty space isn't wasted space — it's breathing room. Cramming every pixel with text and images makes content look amateur and overwhelming. Give your elements room. A slide with 30% white space feels more premium than one with 0%.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Consistency:</strong> Same fonts, same colors, same layout patterns across every post. This builds brand recognition. When someone scrolling sees your content, they should recognize it's yours before reading a word. Pick 2 fonts and 3 colors and use them everywhere.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Thumbnails</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Thumbnails are billboards. Design them for someone scrolling at full speed.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">The thumbnail test: shrink it to the size of a postage stamp. Can you still tell what it's about? If not, it's too complex. Great thumbnails have 3 elements maximum: a face showing emotion, 2-4 words of text, and one contextual visual element. That's it. Every additional element reduces clarity.</p>
  <p class="section-text">AI workflow for thumbnails: Use Midjourney or DALL-E to generate background concepts. Use Canva or Photoshop to composite your face (or a stock face with commercial rights) with the generated background. Add text in a bold, sans-serif font. AI prompt for concepts: "Generate 5 thumbnail concepts for a video titled '[title]'. Each concept should include: the emotion the face should show, the 2-3 words of overlay text, the background color/scene, and one supporting visual element."</p>
  <p class="section-text">A/B test thumbnails whenever possible. YouTube lets you test up to 3 thumbnails simultaneously. On other platforms, post the same content on different days with different thumbnail styles and track which performs better. Data beats assumptions.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Color psychology:</strong> Red/orange create urgency and excitement. Blue builds trust and calm. Yellow grabs attention (used sparingly). Green signals money or growth. Use colors intentionally to trigger the right emotional response for your content.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Carousels</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Instagram carousels get 3x more engagement than single images. Here's the formula.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Carousels outperform every other Instagram format for engagement because the swipe action signals the algorithm that the content is valuable. The key: each slide must earn the next swipe. If someone stops at slide 3 of 10, the algorithm notices.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Slide 1 (The cover):</strong> Bold headline that creates curiosity. This is your hook — it determines whether anyone swipes at all. Use the hook formulas from Lesson 5. <strong>Slides 2-8 (The value):</strong> One point per slide. Large headline, 2-3 lines of supporting text max. Every slide should end with a visual or textual cue to keep swiping ("But here's the thing..." or an arrow). <strong>Slide 9 (The summary):</strong> Quick recap of all points. <strong>Slide 10 (The CTA):</strong> "Save this for later," "Share with someone who needs this," "Follow for more."</p>
  <p class="section-text">AI prompt: "Create a 10-slide Instagram carousel on [topic]. For each slide, provide: the headline (under 8 words), supporting text (under 30 words), and a transition hook to the next slide. Make slide 1 a strong hook, slides 2-8 one point each, slide 9 a summary, and slide 10 a CTA. Keep the tone [your tone]."</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI Design Tools</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The tool stack that replaces a $5,000/month design team.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Canva (free + Pro at $13/mo):</strong> The all-in-one tool for social graphics. AI features include Magic Design (generates layouts from your content), Background Remover, Magic Write (caption generation), and brand kit management. Best for: carousels, stories, quote graphics, and basic thumbnails.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Midjourney / DALL-E ($10-30/mo):</strong> AI image generation for custom visuals. Create unique backgrounds, conceptual illustrations, and style-consistent imagery that beats stock photos. Midjourney excels at photorealistic and artistic images. DALL-E excels at following precise instructions. Best for: thumbnails, hero images, and unique visual branding.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Figma (free tier):</strong> For creators who want more control than Canva but don't want Photoshop. AI plugins like Magician generate icons, copy, and layouts. Best for: custom templates you'll reuse across dozens of posts.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Remove.bg + Photoroom (free):</strong> Instant background removal for product shots, headshots, and composite images. Upload a photo, get a clean cutout in seconds. Essential for thumbnails and professional-looking graphics.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Template strategy:</strong> Design 5 templates (one for each content pillar) and reuse them. Change the text, swap the colors slightly, update the images — but keep the layout consistent. This gives you visual brand consistency while cutting creation time to under 5 minutes per post. AI can generate the initial templates; you refine and reuse.
  </div>
