<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Revenue Stack</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Relying on one income stream is a job. Five income streams is a business.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">The 5 creator revenue streams, in order of when most creators activate them: <strong>1. Platform monetization</strong> (YouTube AdSense, TikTok Creator Fund, Instagram bonuses) — pays $2-7 per 1,000 views. Easy but low. <strong>2. Brand deals/sponsorships</strong> — companies pay you to promote products. The biggest single paycheck most creators receive. <strong>3. Affiliate marketing</strong> — earn commissions recommending products. Amazon Associates, ShareASale, individual brand programs. Passive and scalable.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>4. Digital products</strong> — courses, templates, ebooks, presets, guides. Create once, sell forever. Margins are 90%+. <strong>5. Services/community</strong> — coaching, consulting, paid community access. Highest per-customer revenue but trades time for money unless you scale with AI and group formats.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The magic happens when you stack these. A single video can earn AdSense revenue, include an affiliate link in the description, be sponsored by a brand, drive sales of your digital product, and attract members to your paid community. Five revenue streams from one piece of content. AI helps you manage the complexity.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Brand Deals</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">You don't need a million followers to get paid. You need a pitch.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Brands are shifting budgets from macro-influencers (1M+ followers) to micro-influencers (5K-100K) because engagement rates are 3-4x higher. If you have 5,000+ engaged followers in a clear niche, brands will pay you. The rate formula: $100 per 10,000 followers is the baseline, but niche matters. Finance, tech, and B2B creators can charge 3-5x the baseline because their audience has higher purchasing power.</p>
  <p class="section-text">AI-powered pitch template: "I'm [name], a [niche] content creator with [X] followers and an average engagement rate of [Y]%. My audience is [demographics]. I'm reaching out because [specific reason this brand aligns with my content]. I'd love to create [specific content format] featuring [product]. Here's my media kit: [link]. Would you be open to discussing a collaboration?" AI can customize this for every brand in minutes.</p>
  <p class="section-text">To find brands: look at who's sponsoring creators in your niche. Check their website for "partnerships" or "influencer" pages. Use platforms like AspireIQ, Grin, or CreatorIQ that connect brands with creators. Or go direct — DM the brand's social media manager with a concise pitch. AI can research 50 potential brand partners and draft personalized pitches in under an hour.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The media kit:</strong> Every creator needs one. It's a 1-2 page PDF with: your bio, audience demographics (age, location, interests), engagement metrics, content examples, and past brand collaborations. AI + Canva can generate a professional media kit in 30 minutes. Update it monthly.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Digital Products</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Create once, sell forever. AI makes product creation ridiculously fast.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Digital products are the highest-margin revenue stream: no inventory, no shipping, no cost per unit after creation. The best digital products solve a specific problem your audience already has. You know what that problem is because they tell you in comments, DMs, and emails (Lesson 8's feedback loop).</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Product ideas by niche:</strong> Fitness creators sell workout programs and meal plans. Business creators sell templates and SOPs. Design creators sell Canva templates and presets. Education creators sell courses and study guides. Finance creators sell spreadsheets and planners. AI prompt: "Based on my niche [niche] and the top questions my audience asks [list], suggest 5 digital products I could create. For each: the product format, the price point ($), estimated creation time, and the hook I'd use to sell it."</p>
  <p class="section-text">AI accelerates every step of product creation: outlining the content, writing first drafts, designing templates, creating sales pages, and writing email sequences. A course that used to take 3 months to create can be done in 2-3 weeks with AI handling the structure and first drafts while you add your expertise and personality.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Platforms for selling: Gumroad (simplest), Teachable or Kajabi (courses), Etsy (templates and printables), your own website with Stripe (highest margins, most control). Start with Gumroad — zero upfront cost, they take 10% of sales.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Math</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">What does sustainable creator income actually look like?</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Let's model a creator with 10,000 followers who's stacked all five revenue streams. Platform monetization: $200/month (modest). One brand deal per month: $500. Affiliate income: $300/month (ongoing recommendations). Digital product sales: $1,500/month (selling a $47 template to ~32 people). Paid community: $500/month (25 members at $20). <strong>Total: $3,000/month from 10,000 followers.</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text">Now scale it. At 50,000 followers with the same structure, those numbers roughly 3-5x. At 100,000 followers, brand deal rates jump to $2,000-5,000 each, digital product sales increase with reach, and community membership grows. The key insight: revenue doesn't scale linearly with followers — it scales with the depth of your revenue stack and the strength of your community.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The trap to avoid: chasing followers instead of revenue. A creator with 5,000 followers and a $47 product that converts at 2% per month makes $4,700/month. A creator with 100,000 followers and no product makes $300/month from AdSense. Build the business infrastructure alongside the audience, not after.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The 1,000 true fans theory:</strong> Kevin Kelly's framework holds true. If 1,000 people pay you $100/year (through products, community, or services), that's $100,000/year. You don't need millions of followers. You need 1,000 people who love what you do enough to pay for it. AI helps you serve those 1,000 people at a level that keeps them paying.
  </div>
</div>
