<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Product Mindset</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Services trade time for money. Products trade creation for money.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Every service project you complete earns money once. Every product you create earns money every time someone buys it. That's the fundamental shift. And AI just collapsed the creation time from weeks to hours.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Two categories dominate the AI product space right now: <strong>print-on-demand</strong> (physical products with zero inventory) and <strong>digital products</strong> (files delivered instantly with zero shipping). Both have near-zero startup costs and infinite scalability.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The numbers are real. Etsy alone processed $13.2 billion in gross merchandise sales in 2024. Gumroad creators earned over $800 million collectively. You don't need a massive audience — you need a specific product that solves a specific problem for a specific person.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Print-on-Demand</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Zero inventory. Zero shipping. Real revenue.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Print-on-demand (POD) means you upload a design, someone orders a product with your design, and a third party prints and ships it. You never touch inventory. Your profit is the markup between the base cost and your selling price.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>The platform stack:</strong></p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Printful + Etsy:</strong> The beginner combo. Printful handles printing and shipping. Etsy provides the marketplace and traffic. A t-shirt costs ~$12 to print, sells for $25-$35. Your margin: $13-$23 per sale.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Redbubble & TeePublic:</strong> Even simpler — upload designs, they handle everything. Lower margins ($3-$8 per sale) but zero marketing required. Good for testing designs.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Amazon Merch:</strong> Massive traffic, invitation-only. Apply and wait. Once approved, you're accessing 300 million+ active customers.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>AI Design Workflow:</strong> Use Midjourney or Ideogram for design generation. Prompt formula: "[style] [subject] [modifier], isolated on [background], vector art, high contrast, printable." Example: "minimalist geometric cat, isolated on black background, vector art, high contrast, t-shirt ready." Upscale to 4500x5400px for print quality using Topaz Gigapixel or Upscayl (free).
  </div>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Winning niches:</strong> Niche audiences buy merch. "Dog mom" is saturated. "Bernese Mountain Dog mom who hikes" is a goldmine. Use Etsy search and eRank to find niches with demand but limited competition. Target 500-2,000 monthly searches with fewer than 500 competing listings.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Digital Products</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Files that sell for $10-$200 with 95% profit margins.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Digital products are pure profit after creation. No printing, no shipping, no inventory. Here are the five digital product types crushing it right now:</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>1. Notion Templates ($5-$50):</strong> Project trackers, content calendars, habit trackers, business dashboards. Use AI to generate the structure, then build in Notion. Sell on Gumroad or Notion's template marketplace. Top creators sell 500+ templates/month.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>2. Prompt Packs ($10-$50):</strong> Curated, tested prompts for specific professions. "200 ChatGPT Prompts for Real Estate Agents" or "AI Prompts for Etsy Sellers." Package as a PDF with examples and expected outputs.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>3. Canva Template Bundles ($15-$100):</strong> Social media templates, presentation decks, brand kits. Use AI to generate copy and design concepts, then execute in Canva. Bundle 50+ templates together for higher perceived value.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>4. Educational Guides ($20-$100):</strong> Step-by-step guides for specific outcomes. "How to Start a Podcast in 2026" or "The Complete Guide to AI for Small Business." AI drafts the content; you add expertise and formatting.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>5. Spreadsheet Tools ($10-$75):</strong> Budget trackers, pricing calculators, inventory systems in Google Sheets or Excel. Use AI to write complex formulas and macros. Sell to small businesses on AppSumo or Gumroad.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Launch Playbook</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Create in a day. Launch in a week. Iterate forever.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 1-2:</strong> Research your niche. Find 3-5 products already selling well. Study their reviews — what do buyers love? What do they complain about? Build something that solves the complaints.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 3-4:</strong> Create your product using AI. A Notion template takes 2-3 hours. A prompt pack takes 4-6 hours. A Canva bundle takes 6-8 hours. Quality matters — make it genuinely useful.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 5-6:</strong> Write your listing. Use AI to generate the title, description, and tags. Your listing title should include the primary keyword buyers search for. Add mockup images — Placeit.net or Canva mockup generator.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 7:</strong> Launch. Price competitively for your first product (slightly below the market leader). Ask 3-5 friends to buy and leave honest reviews. Iterate based on feedback. Rinse and repeat with a new product every 1-2 weeks.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>The compounding effect:</strong> 10 products at $20 each, averaging 2 sales per day across all products = $40/day = $1,200/month. At 25 products, that's $3,000/month. Every new product is a permanent addition to your revenue stream.</p>
</div>
