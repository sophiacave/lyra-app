<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Content Economy</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Every business needs content. Almost none of them can make it themselves.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">There are 33 million small businesses in the US alone. Most know they need a blog, a newsletter, and social media presence. Almost none have the time or skill to execute consistently. That gap is your opportunity.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Before AI, a solo content creator could realistically produce 3-5 polished blog posts per week. With AI in your workflow, you can produce 10-15 at the same quality. That's not about replacing your brain — it's about eliminating the blank page, speeding up research, and automating the boring parts.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The three monetization models that work:
  <strong>Client Services</strong> — you write for businesses ($0.15-$0.50/word). <strong>Your Own Platform</strong> — you build an audience and monetize through ads, affiliates, or sponsorships ($500-$5,000/month at scale). <strong>Content Products</strong> — templates, swipe files, and content calendars sold as digital products ($20-$200 each).</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The AI Content Workflow</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your new process: Research, Generate, Edit, Publish.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Step 1 — Research (5 min):</strong> Use Claude or ChatGPT to analyze top-ranking articles for your target keyword. Prompt: "Analyze the top 5 articles about [topic]. What do they all cover? What do they miss?" This gives you a content gap to exploit.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Step 2 — Generate (10 min):</strong> Feed your AI a detailed brief: target audience, tone, key points, word count, and the gaps you found. Never just say "write a blog post about X." The more context you give, the less editing you do.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Step 3 — Edit (15 min):</strong> This is where you earn your money. Add personal experience, specific examples, data points, and opinions AI can't have. Rewrite the intro to hook readers. Cut filler. A good editor turns AI output from a C+ to an A.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Step 4 — Publish & Repurpose (5 min):</strong> One blog post becomes 3-5 social posts, a newsletter section, and a thread. Use AI to reformat: "Turn this 1,500-word blog post into 5 LinkedIn posts, each under 200 words, with a hook in the first line."</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Pro move:</strong> Build a prompt library. Save your best-performing prompts in a Notion database. After 30 days you'll have a system that produces consistent quality with minimal thinking. That prompt library itself becomes a sellable product.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Blog Monetization</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">SEO + AI = traffic you don't pay for.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">The fastest path to blog income: pick a niche with commercial intent (people searching to buy something), write AI-assisted SEO content, monetize with affiliate links. Tools like Ahrefs, Ubersuggest, or even free Google Keyword Planner show you exactly what people search for.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Target keywords with 500-5,000 monthly searches and low competition. Write 2-3 posts per week. At month 3-4, traffic starts compounding. By month 6, a focused niche site can generate $500-$2,000/month in affiliate revenue alone.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Real example: A site reviewing AI tools (ironic, right?) publishing 3 AI-assisted articles per week reached 50,000 monthly visitors in 5 months. Affiliate commissions from tools like Jasper, Writesonic, and Midjourney averaged $2,300/month because those tools pay 20-30% recurring commissions.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Newsletter Money</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Email lists are the only asset you truly own.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Social platforms change algorithms daily. Google updates kill sites overnight. But your email list? That's yours. Newsletters are the most underrated income stream for AI-powered creators.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>The stack:</strong> Beehiiv or ConvertKit (free tier to start), AI for drafting, and a consistent schedule. Monetize three ways: sponsorships (once you hit 1,000+ subscribers), paid tiers ($5-$15/month), and promoting your own products.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Growth hack:</strong> Use AI to write a "lead magnet" — a free PDF, checklist, or mini-course that people get when they subscribe. "The 50 Best AI Prompts for [Industry]" converts at 30-40% on a landing page. Pair it with $5/day Facebook ads targeted at your niche and you're adding 10-20 subscribers daily for $0.25-$0.50 each.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The math: 2,000 subscribers, 3% convert to paid at $10/month = $600/month recurring. Plus one sponsorship slot at $200-$500 per issue, twice monthly. You're looking at $1,000-$1,600/month from a weekly newsletter that takes 2 hours to produce with AI.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Landing Clients</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your first client is closer than you think.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Don't start on Upwork. Start local. Every dentist, plumber, real estate agent, and restaurant in your area needs content and has zero time for it. Walk in with a sample blog post you wrote about their industry using AI. Offer the first one free. Price the rest at $150-$300 per post.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Three clients at 4 posts/month each = 12 posts = roughly 6 hours of work with AI = $1,800-$3,600/month. That's your Tier 1 baseline, and you can build it in 2-3 weeks.</p>
</div>
