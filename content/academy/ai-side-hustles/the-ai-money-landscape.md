<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Reality Check</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Most "AI side hustle" advice is garbage. Here's what actually works.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Scroll TikTok for 30 seconds and someone's promising you $10K/month with ChatGPT. The truth? Most of those creators make money selling courses about making money. The actual AI economy is different — it's quieter, more technical, and way more sustainable.</p>
  <p class="section-text">Here's the real breakdown. The AI side hustle market falls into three tiers based on skill level and earning potential:</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Tier 1 — Content & Services ($500-$2K/month):</strong> AI-assisted writing, social media management, image generation for small businesses. Low barrier to entry, high competition. You're competing with everyone who watched the same YouTube tutorial.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Tier 2 — Specialized Skills ($2K-$8K/month):</strong> AI automation consulting, prompt engineering for enterprises, custom chatbot development. Requires domain knowledge plus AI skills. This is the sweet spot.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Tier 3 — Products & Scale ($5K-$50K+/month):</strong> Micro-SaaS tools, AI-powered digital products, automation agencies. Requires building something once and selling it many times. Hardest to start, highest ceiling.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Hype Filter</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Five questions that kill 90% of bad ideas.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">Before you chase any AI opportunity, run it through this filter. If it fails two or more, walk away.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The 5-Question Hype Filter:</strong>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Who's paying?</strong> If you can't name a specific buyer persona, it's a fantasy.</li>
      <li><strong>What's the pain?</strong> Real businesses pay to solve pain, not to be impressed by AI.</li>
      <li><strong>Can AI actually do this well?</strong> AI-generated novels? Mediocre. AI-generated product descriptions? Excellent.</li>
      <li><strong>What's my moat?</strong> If anyone with ChatGPT can do it, the price races to zero.</li>
      <li><strong>Can I deliver in under a week?</strong> Long projects kill side hustles. Fast delivery = fast cash.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
  <p class="section-text">Real example: "AI logo design" sounds great until you realize Canva and DALL-E give everyone the same capability. But "AI-powered brand identity systems for restaurants" — that's specific, painful, and defensible. The niche is the moat.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">What's Working Right Now</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The six proven models generating real revenue in 2026.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>1. AI Content Production.</strong> Blogs, newsletters, social posts. Tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Jasper handle first drafts. You add expertise, editing, and strategy. Typical rate: $0.10-$0.50/word or $500-$2,000/month per client.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>2. AI-Powered Freelancing.</strong> Use AI to 3x your output on Upwork or Fiverr. A copywriter using AI writes 5,000 words/day instead of 1,500. Same quality, triple the revenue. Top freelancers report $5K-$15K/month.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>3. Automation Consulting.</strong> Small businesses waste 10-20 hours/week on repetitive tasks. You build Make.com, Zapier, or n8n workflows that eliminate that waste. Charge $500-$5,000 per automation. Recurring maintenance fees of $200-$500/month.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>4. Digital Products.</strong> Templates, prompt libraries, Notion systems, Canva bundles — all created with AI assistance. Sell on Gumroad, Etsy, or your own site. $10-$100 per product, scales infinitely.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>5. Micro-SaaS.</strong> Tiny software tools built with AI coding assistants like Cursor, Claude Code, or GitHub Copilot. Solve one problem for one audience. Charge $9-$49/month. Even 100 users at $29/month = $2,900 recurring.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>6. AI Tutoring & Coaching.</strong> Teach people how to use AI for their specific job. A real estate agent who learns to use AI for listings will pay $200/hour for that training. You don't need to be an AI researcher — just better than your client.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Your Starting Point</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Pick one model. Master it. Then stack.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">The biggest mistake beginners make is trying everything at once. You end up with five half-built projects and zero revenue. Instead, pick the model that matches your existing skills:</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>You're a writer?</strong> Start with AI content production. Lesson 2 covers this.</p>
    <p><strong>You're technical?</strong> Jump to automation consulting (Lesson 7) or micro-SaaS (Lesson 5).</p>
    <p><strong>You're a people person?</strong> AI tutoring (Lesson 6) or freelancing (Lesson 3) is your lane.</p>
    <p><strong>You want passive income?</strong> Digital products (Lesson 4) build slow but compound.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="section-text">Every model in this course has been validated by real people earning real money. No theory. No hype. Just a playbook you can execute this week.</p>
  <p class="section-text">The tools you'll use throughout this course: ChatGPT or Claude for content, Midjourney or DALL-E for images, Make.com or n8n for automation, Cursor or Replit for building, and Stripe or Gumroad for getting paid. All of them have free tiers.</p>
