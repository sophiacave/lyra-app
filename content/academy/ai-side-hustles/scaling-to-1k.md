<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Fastest Paths</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Three roads to $1K. Pick the one that matches your DNA.</h2>
  <p class="section-text">You've learned the landscape. Now it's execution time. Based on everything in this course, here are the three fastest paths to your first $1,000:</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Path A — The Service Sprint (Best for: people with writing, marketing, or admin skills):</strong> Land 2-3 content or VA clients at $400-$500 each. Total timeline: 2-3 weeks. This is the fastest path because you're selling a skill people already understand.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Path B — The Automation Build (Best for: people with technical curiosity):</strong> Complete 1-2 automation projects at $500-$1,000 each. Timeline: 3-4 weeks. Slightly slower start but higher per-project revenue and built-in recurring potential.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Path C — The Product Play (Best for: people who want passive income):</strong> Create and launch 3-5 digital products averaging $15-$30 each, target 40-70 sales. Timeline: 4-6 weeks. Slowest to start but every sale is pure profit with zero additional work.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <p><strong>Can't decide?</strong> Go with Path A. It's the fastest to first dollar, builds your portfolio, and teaches you what clients actually need — which informs everything else you build later.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Week 1: Foundation</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Days 1-7: Set up everything. Tell everyone.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 1 — Choose your niche and model.</strong> Pick one industry (real estate, e-commerce, health & wellness, etc.) and one service (content, automation, or products). Write a one-sentence pitch: "I help [audience] do [outcome] using AI tools."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 2 — Set up your tools.</strong> Create accounts on Claude or ChatGPT (for content), Make.com (for automation), or Gumroad (for products). Get a professional email address. Set up a simple portfolio page on Carrd ($19/year) or Notion (free). You don't need a fancy website. You need proof you exist.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Day 3 — Create 2-3 sample deliverables.</strong> Write a blog post for a fictional client in your niche. Build a sample automation. Design a sample product. These are your proof of skill. No samples = no credibility.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 4-5 — Announce to your network.</strong> Post on LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and text/email 20 people you know. Use the script: "I just launched an AI-powered [service] for [niche]. I'm offering my first 3 clients a 30% launch discount. Know anyone who could use this?" Direct, specific, shareable.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 6-7 — Send 20 cold outreach messages.</strong> Use the cold email template from Lesson 9. Target small businesses in your niche. Find them on Google Maps, Yelp, or Instagram. Personalize every message. 20 messages at a 5% response rate = 1 conversation. Send 20 more on Monday.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Week 2: First Revenue</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Days 8-14: Close your first deal. Deliver something exceptional.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 8-9 — Follow up on every conversation.</strong> Most sales happen on the follow-up, not the first touch. Send a brief check-in to everyone who showed interest. "Just wanted to follow up — I had a slot open up this week and could start immediately."</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 10-11 — Close with a free discovery call.</strong> Offer a 20-minute call to anyone on the fence. Use the Automation Audit framework from Lesson 7 or the AAAA Method from Lesson 6, depending on your service. Show value before asking for money.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 12-14 — Deliver your first project.</strong> Go above and beyond. If they bought 1 blog post, deliver 1 blog post plus a bonus social media caption set. If they bought an automation, include a Loom video walkthrough. First impressions create referrals. Make this one count.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Mindset note:</strong> Your first project will feel underpriced. That's fine. The goal of project #1 isn't maximum revenue — it's a testimonial, a case study, and proof that your system works. Project #3 is where you start charging real rates.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Weeks 3-4: Scale to $1K</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Days 15-30: Stack clients, systemize delivery, hit $1,000.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 15-18 — Activate the referral engine.</strong> Follow the three-week sequence from Lesson 9 with your first client. Ask for a testimonial and 1-2 referrals. Post the testimonial on your portfolio and social media.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 19-22 — Send 40 more outreach messages.</strong> Double your volume. You now have a testimonial and a completed project to reference. Your close rate will increase. Target: 2-3 more conversations, 1-2 more closes.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 23-26 — Systemize your delivery.</strong> Turn your process into a repeatable system. Create templates for onboarding emails, project briefs, delivery packages, and follow-up sequences. Every system you build saves hours on future projects.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Days 27-30 — Close the gap to $1K.</strong> By now you should have 2-4 completed or in-progress projects. If you're short of $1K, add a digital product — a prompt pack or template based on your niche expertise. Launch it on Gumroad with a post to your network. Even $100-$200 in product sales fills the gap.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Beyond $1K</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The compounding playbook: $1K to $5K in 90 days.</h2>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Month 2:</strong> Raise prices 20%. Move at least one client to a retainer. Launch your first recurring product (a membership, template subscription, or maintenance package). Target: $2,000-$2,500.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Month 3:</strong> Add your second service. If you started with content, add automation. If you started with automation, add tutoring. Cross-sell to existing clients — they already trust you. Target: $3,500-$5,000.</p>
  <p class="section-text"><strong>Month 4+:</strong> Systematize, delegate, or build products. At this point you've proven the model. The question shifts from "Can I make money with AI?" to "How much do I want to make?" The answer depends entirely on how much time and energy you invest in scaling. Every system from this course — the Hype Filter, the Proposal Formula, the AAAA Method, the Referral Engine — compounds over time.</p>
  <p class="section-text">You have everything you need. The tools are free or cheap. The knowledge is in these lessons. The market is hungry. The only variable left is you.</p>
</div>
