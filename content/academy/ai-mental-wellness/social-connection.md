  <span class="section-label">Disclaimer</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Critical context.</h2>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>AI is not a substitute for human connection or professional support.</strong> Chronic loneliness and social isolation are serious health risks. If you are experiencing persistent loneliness, depression, or social anxiety that limits your life, please seek professional mental health support. AI is a bridge to connection — not the destination.

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Loneliness Paradox</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">We're more connected and more alone than ever.</h2>
  <p>Loneliness is an epidemic. The U.S. Surgeon General has called it a public health crisis on par with smoking and obesity. And it's not just about being alone — you can feel profoundly lonely in a crowded room. Loneliness is the gap between the connection you want and the connection you have.</p>
  <p>AI sits in a tricky spot here. It can feel like connection — a conversation partner who listens, responds, and never judges. But it's not connection. It's simulation. And if AI becomes your primary social outlet, you might feel less lonely in the moment while becoming more isolated over time.</p>
  <p>The goal of this lesson is clear: use AI to build bridges to real human connection, not as a replacement for it.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI as Bridge</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Using AI to get to humans.</h2>
  <p>Here's where AI genuinely helps with loneliness — not by being your friend, but by removing the barriers that keep you from finding friends:</p>
  <p><strong>Social anxiety practice:</strong> Nervous about conversations? Practice with AI first. "Pretend you're someone at a coffee shop. I'm going to practice small talk." AI provides a zero-stakes environment to build confidence before real interactions.</p>
  <p><strong>Finding your people:</strong> Ask AI to help you identify communities, groups, meetups, and organizations that match your interests in your area. "I like board games, I'm introverted, and I live in Portland. Where could I meet people without it being overwhelming?"</p>
  <p><strong>Conversation starters:</strong> AI can help you prepare for social situations. "I'm going to a work happy hour and I don't know what to talk about. Give me five conversation starters that aren't about the weather."</p>
  <p><strong>Processing social interactions:</strong> After a social event, use AI to debrief. "I felt awkward when X happened. Help me figure out what I could do differently next time — or if I'm overthinking it." Often, it's the latter.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The bridge rule:</strong> Every AI conversation about loneliness should end with an action step that involves a real human. "Based on what we talked about, what is one small thing I could do this week to connect with someone?"
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Risks</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">When AI companionship becomes a trap.</h2>
  <p>This needs honest discussion. AI companionship can become problematic when:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>You prefer AI to humans.</strong> If talking to AI feels "easier" than real people, that's a sign to examine, not celebrate. Real connection requires vulnerability, conflict, and imperfection — and that's what makes it meaningful.</li>
    <li><strong>You're substituting, not supplementing.</strong> AI companionship at 2 AM when no one else is awake is healthy. AI companionship instead of calling a friend back is avoidance.</li>
    <li><strong>You're forming emotional attachment to AI.</strong> If you feel like AI "understands" you in ways humans don't, pause. AI generates responses that sound empathetic because that's what the patterns predict. It doesn't actually understand you.</li>
    <li><strong>Your human contact is shrinking.</strong> Track it honestly. If your human interactions are decreasing as your AI interactions increase, course-correct immediately.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The litmus test:</strong> Is AI making your human relationships better, or is it replacing them? If the answer is replacing, something needs to change.
  </div>
</div>
