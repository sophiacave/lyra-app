<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Why AI Meditation</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Beyond the one-size-fits-all meditation app.</h2>
  <p>Meditation apps are great. But they're pre-recorded. They don't know you had a panic attack at work, that you're grieving a breakup, or that you only have 90 seconds before your next meeting. AI does — if you tell it.</p>
  <p>AI-guided mindfulness is dynamic. You tell it exactly what you're feeling, how much time you have, and what you need. It creates a practice for that specific moment. That's not replacing Calm or Headspace — it's filling the gaps those apps can't reach.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Try this right now:</strong> Tell AI: "I have 2 minutes and I'm anxious about a meeting that starts soon. Guide me through a quick grounding exercise." Notice how different this feels from scrolling through a library of pre-made meditations.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Five Practices</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your AI mindfulness toolkit.</h2>
  <p><strong>1. The Situational Calm-Down:</strong> Tell AI exactly what's happening and ask for a 1-3 minute exercise matched to your situation. "I'm in my car after a hard conversation. I need to calm down before I drive." AI creates something specific to that moment.</p>
  <p><strong>2. The Guided Body Scan:</strong> Ask AI to walk you through a progressive body scan at your pace. "Start at my feet, move slowly upward, and pause at each area for 10 seconds." You control the speed and focus — no more skipping ahead on a recording.</p>
  <p><strong>3. The Breathing Coach:</strong> Tell AI your stress level (1-10) and ask for a breathing pattern. High stress? It might suggest 4-7-8 breathing. Moderate? Box breathing. Low? Simple awareness breathing. It matches the technique to your state.</p>
  <p><strong>4. The Thought Observer:</strong> When your mind won't stop racing, tell AI every thought bouncing around your head. Then ask: "Help me observe these thoughts without attaching to them." AI guides you through cognitive defusion — noticing thoughts as clouds passing, not facts to react to.</p>
  <p><strong>5. The Evening Wind-Down:</strong> Before bed, ask AI for a 5-minute guided relaxation that addresses your day. "I had a stressful day involving conflict with my boss. Help me release this before sleep." Personalized to what actually happened.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Breathwork Deep Dive</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Three breathing patterns AI can guide you through.</h2>
  <p><strong>Box Breathing (4-4-4-4):</strong> Inhale 4 seconds, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Best for: general stress, pre-performance calm. Ask AI to count you through it.</p>
  <p><strong>4-7-8 Breathing:</strong> Inhale 4 seconds, hold 7, exhale 8. Best for: high anxiety, insomnia, panic. The long exhale activates your parasympathetic nervous system.</p>
  <p><strong>Physiological Sigh:</strong> Double inhale through the nose (short + long), then long exhale through the mouth. Best for: acute stress in the moment. It's the fastest way to calm your nervous system — research from Stanford confirms this.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Voice mode is your friend:</strong> If your AI tool has voice capabilities, use them for meditation. Closing your eyes and listening is far more effective than reading text off a screen. You're trying to disconnect from screens, not stare at one harder.
