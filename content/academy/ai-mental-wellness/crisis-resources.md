  <h2 class="section-title">Stop reading and use these.</h2>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>CRISIS RESOURCES — SAVE THESE:</strong><br><br>
    <strong>988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:</strong> Call or text <strong>988</strong> (24/7, free, confidential). Supports anyone in suicidal crisis, emotional distress, or substance use crisis. Available in English and Spanish. For Deaf/Hard of Hearing: use your preferred relay service or dial 711 then 988.<br><br>
    <strong>Crisis Text Line:</strong> Text <strong>HOME</strong> to <strong>741741</strong> (24/7, free, confidential). Connects you with a trained crisis counselor via text. For anyone in any type of crisis.<br><br>
    <strong>Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ youth):</strong> Call <strong>1-866-488-7386</strong> or text <strong>START</strong> to <strong>678-678</strong> (24/7).<br><br>
    <strong>Trans Lifeline:</strong> Call <strong>877-565-8860</strong> (staffed by trans people, for trans people).<br><br>
    <strong>SAMHSA National Helpline:</strong> Call <strong>1-800-662-4357</strong> (24/7, free referrals for substance use and mental health).<br><br>
    <strong>National Domestic Violence Hotline:</strong> Call <strong>1-800-799-7233</strong> or text <strong>START</strong> to <strong>88788</strong>.<br><br>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI Cannot Handle This</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The line between AI help and human help.</h2>
  <p><strong>AI is NOT equipped to handle mental health crises.</strong> This is not a limitation we're working on — it's a fundamental boundary. Here's why:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>AI cannot assess risk.</strong> It cannot tell if you're in immediate danger. A trained crisis counselor can.</li>
    <li><strong>AI cannot call for help.</strong> If you need emergency services, AI cannot dispatch them. A hotline can.</li>
    <li><strong>AI cannot provide the human connection that saves lives.</strong> In a crisis, knowing a real person cares matters. AI's empathy is simulated.</li>
    <li><strong>AI can say the wrong thing.</strong> AI responses are generated by patterns, not clinical training. In a crisis, the wrong response can be dangerous.</li>
    <li><strong>AI conversations may not be private.</strong> Your crisis disclosure might be stored and reviewed. Crisis hotlines are legally confidential.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The hard rule:</strong> If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, thinking about suicide or self-harm, or experiencing a psychiatric emergency — close the AI, and call 988 or 911. This is not negotiable.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Recognizing a Crisis</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Signs it's time to reach for human help.</h2>
  <p>Not every hard day is a crisis. But some are. Here's how to know the difference:</p>
  <p><strong>In yourself:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Thoughts of suicide or self-harm, even if they feel "passive" or "not serious"</li>
    <li>Feeling like others would be better off without you</li>
    <li>Giving away possessions or saying goodbye to people</li>
    <li>Sudden calm after a period of intense depression (this can signal a plan has been made)</li>
    <li>Feeling completely hopeless with no ability to see a future</li>
    <li>Hearing or seeing things that aren't there</li>
    <li>Unable to care for yourself — not eating, not sleeping for days, not functioning</li>
  </ul>
  <p><strong>In someone you care about:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Talking about wanting to die or being a burden</li>
    <li>Withdrawing from everyone suddenly</li>
    <li>Dramatic mood changes — especially sudden calmness after depression</li>
    <li>Increased substance use</li>
    <li>Researching methods or accessing means</li>
  </ul>
  <p>If any of these apply: call 988, text HOME to 741741, or go to an emergency room. You are not overreacting.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Finding Professional Help</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Beyond crisis — getting ongoing support.</h2>
  <p>Crisis lines are for emergencies. But you also deserve ongoing support. AI can actually help you find it:</p>
  <p><strong>Ask AI to help you search:</strong> "Help me find a therapist in [your city] who specializes in [your concern] and accepts [your insurance]." AI can help you navigate the confusing landscape of finding a provider.</p>
  <p><strong>Sliding scale and free options:</strong> Ask AI about community mental health centers, university training clinics (lower cost, supervised students), Open Path Collective (sessions for $30-$80), and SAMHSA's treatment locator.</p>
  <p><strong>Overcoming barriers:</strong> Tell AI what's stopping you — cost, stigma, time, not knowing where to start — and ask for specific solutions. Sometimes the barrier is just the phone call. AI can even help you script what to say when you call a therapist's office for the first time.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>You deserve help.</strong> Seeking professional support is not weakness. It's the same as seeing a doctor for a broken bone. Your brain is part of your body, and it deserves care too.
  </div>
</div>
