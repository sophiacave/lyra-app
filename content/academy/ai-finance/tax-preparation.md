<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Tax Basics</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Understanding taxes without the headache.</h2>
  <p>Taxes are confusing by design. But AI can translate tax jargon into plain English faster than any textbook. Here's where to start:</p>
  <p><strong>Tax brackets:</strong> Ask AI to explain how marginal tax brackets work. The number one misconception: "If I earn more, I'll lose money to a higher bracket." That's not how it works, and AI can show you exactly why with your own income numbers.</p>
  <p><strong>Standard vs. itemized deductions:</strong> Most people take the standard deduction. But sometimes itemizing saves more. AI can help you estimate both and compare — though you should verify with a tax professional.</p>
  <p><strong>Credits vs. deductions:</strong> A tax credit reduces your tax bill dollar-for-dollar. A deduction reduces your taxable income. A $1,000 credit is worth more than a $1,000 deduction. AI can explain which credits you might qualify for.</p>
  <p><strong>Self-employment taxes:</strong> If you freelance or have a side business, you owe self-employment tax on top of income tax. AI can estimate your self-employment tax liability so it doesn't surprise you at filing time.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Great AI prompt:</strong> "Explain how US federal income tax brackets work. Use a $65,000 salary as an example and show me exactly how much goes to each bracket. What's my effective tax rate vs. marginal rate?"
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Year-Round</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Tax prep is a year-round habit, not an April event.</h2>
  <p>People who scramble in April miss deductions, make mistakes, and overpay. People who track year-round file accurately and confidently. AI makes year-round tracking almost effortless.</p>
  <p><strong>Monthly: Categorize expenses.</strong> If you're self-employed or have deductible expenses, categorize them monthly using AI. Export your transactions and ask AI to flag anything potentially deductible. Don't wait until you have 12 months of uncategorized transactions.</p>
  <p><strong>Quarterly: Estimate taxes.</strong> Self-employed? Use AI to estimate your quarterly tax payments based on year-to-date income. Underpaying quarterlies can trigger penalties.</p>
  <p><strong>Annually: Gather documents.</strong> By mid-January, use AI to create a checklist of every tax document you need: W-2s, 1099s, interest statements, property tax records, charitable donation receipts. Track what's arrived and what's missing.</p>
  <p><strong>Filing season: Review before filing.</strong> Use AI to review your completed return for common errors: math mistakes, missing forms, overlooked deductions. This is a sanity check, not a substitute for professional review.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The receipt system:</strong> Take a photo of every receipt for potentially deductible expenses. Store them in a folder organized by month. At tax time, AI can help you categorize them from the photos. It takes 5 seconds per receipt and saves hours of searching later.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Deductions</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Commonly missed deductions AI can help you find.</h2>
  <p>People leave money on the table every year because they don't know what's deductible. AI can review your situation and flag potential deductions — but always confirm eligibility with a tax professional.</p>
  <p><strong>Home office:</strong> If you work from home, a portion of your rent/mortgage, utilities, and internet may be deductible. AI can calculate the percentage based on your office square footage vs. total home size.</p>
  <p><strong>Education expenses:</strong> Courses, certifications, and books related to your current profession may be deductible for self-employed individuals. This course might qualify — ask your tax advisor.</p>
  <p><strong>Health insurance premiums:</strong> Self-employed individuals may be able to deduct health insurance premiums. AI can explain the requirements and limitations.</p>
  <p><strong>Mileage and travel:</strong> Business-related driving and travel can be deductible. AI can calculate deductions based on the standard mileage rate for the current tax year.</p>
  <p><strong>Charitable donations:</strong> Including non-cash donations like clothing and household items. AI can help you estimate fair market value, but keep documentation.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The AI deduction finder:</strong> Describe your work situation, income sources, and major expenses to AI. Ask: "Based on this information, what deductions should I discuss with my tax professional?" Use the list as a starting point for a conversation with your CPA.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">DIY vs. Pro</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">When to hire a professional.</h2>
  <p><strong>DIY is fine if:</strong> You have a single W-2, no major life changes, no investments beyond a standard retirement account, and you take the standard deduction. Tax software handles this well.</p>
  <p><strong>Hire a professional if:</strong> You're self-employed, have multiple income sources, own rental property, experienced a major life event (marriage, divorce, home purchase), have complex investments, or are dealing with back taxes. The cost of a CPA is usually much less than the errors and missed deductions of DIY.</p>
  <p>AI helps in both scenarios. For DIY filers, it explains what each form means and catches errors. For those working with professionals, it helps you prepare organized records so your CPA spends less time (and charges less) on your return.</p>
</div>
