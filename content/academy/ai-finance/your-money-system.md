<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The System</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">One system. Four layers. Total clarity.</h2>
  <p>Throughout this course, you've learned individual skills: budgeting, debt management, investing basics, credit building, taxes, and more. Now we wire them all together into a single system that takes minimal time and gives maximum clarity.</p>
  <p>Your AI-powered money system has four layers:</p>
  <p><strong>Layer 1: Tracking.</strong> Automated expense categorization and income logging. This is your data foundation. Without accurate tracking, everything else is guesswork.</p>
  <p><strong>Layer 2: Planning.</strong> Your budget, debt payoff plan, savings targets, and financial goals. These are your guardrails.</p>
  <p><strong>Layer 3: Optimization.</strong> Credit monitoring, tax preparation, and finding efficiency in your spending. This is where AI shines — spotting patterns and opportunities you'd miss.</p>
  <p><strong>Layer 4: Growth.</strong> Retirement contributions, investment education, and building wealth over time. This is the long game.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Start with layers 1 and 2.</strong> Get tracking and planning solid before worrying about optimization and growth. A strong foundation supports everything above it.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Rituals</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The financial rituals that keep your system alive.</h2>
  <p>A system without rituals is just a plan that dies in a drawer. Here's the cadence that keeps everything running:</p>
  <p><strong>Weekly (10 minutes, Sunday):</strong></p>
  <p>- Review categorized spending for the week</p>
  <p>- Check budget vs. actual for each category</p>
  <p>- Log any cash transactions or manual entries</p>
  <p>- One AI prompt: "Summarize my spending this week and flag anything unusual."</p>
  <p><strong>Monthly (30 minutes, 1st of the month):</strong></p>
  <p>- Full monthly spending review with AI analysis</p>
  <p>- Update debt balances and track payoff progress</p>
  <p>- Review subscriptions and recurring charges</p>
  <p>- Calculate and record net worth</p>
  <p>- One AI prompt: "Compare this month's spending to last month. Highlight trends and any categories that grew more than 10%."</p>
  <p><strong>Quarterly (1 hour):</strong></p>
  <p>- Comprehensive financial health check</p>
  <p>- Credit report review</p>
  <p>- Retirement contribution check</p>
  <p>- Tax estimate update (if self-employed)</p>
  <p>- Goal progress review</p>
  <p>- One AI prompt: "Based on my last 3 months of data, give me a financial health report card. Score me on spending control, debt reduction, savings rate, and progress toward goals."</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The non-negotiable:</strong> The weekly 10-minute check is the anchor. If you do nothing else, do this. It's the difference between financial awareness and financial blindness.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Prompt Library</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Build your personal AI finance prompt library.</h2>
  <p>The best AI users don't write prompts from scratch every time. They build and refine a library. Here are the prompts that power your money system:</p>
  <p><strong>Weekly review:</strong> "Here are my transactions for the past week [paste]. Categorize each, total by category, compare to my budget targets of [list targets], and flag overspending."</p>
  <p><strong>Monthly analysis:</strong> "Here are my transactions for [month]. Generate a spending report with: total income, total expenses, savings amount, savings rate, top 5 categories, and month-over-month comparison."</p>
  <p><strong>Debt progress:</strong> "My current debts are [list with balances and rates]. I'm paying [amount] extra per month using the [avalanche/snowball] method. When will each debt be paid off? How much interest will I save?"</p>
  <p><strong>Net worth update:</strong> "My assets: [list]. My liabilities: [list]. Calculate my net worth and compare to last month's net worth of [amount]. What changed and why?"</p>
  <p><strong>Financial decision:</strong> "I'm considering [purchase/decision]. It costs [amount]. Here's my current financial situation [summary]. What are the pros, cons, and financial impact?"</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Save these prompts.</strong> Keep them in a note or document you can access quickly. Refine them over time as you learn what information AI needs to give you the most useful output.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">90-Day Plan</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your next 90 days.</h2>
  <p>Knowledge without action is just trivia. Here's your action plan:</p>
  <p><strong>Week 1:</strong> Export last month's transactions. Run them through AI for categorization and analysis. Set up your tracking system (spreadsheet or app). Calculate your net worth.</p>
  <p><strong>Week 2:</strong> Create your budget based on real spending data. Set up your debt inventory if applicable. Start the weekly 10-minute review habit.</p>
  <p><strong>Week 3-4:</strong> Pull your credit report. Run it through AI for analysis. Set up credit monitoring. Identify one credit improvement action and take it.</p>
  <p><strong>Month 2:</strong> Review your first full month of tracked data. Adjust your budget based on reality. If you have debt, choose your payoff strategy and start. If not, review your savings rate and set a target.</p>
  <p><strong>Month 3:</strong> Do your first quarterly review. Calculate 3-month trends. Evaluate your retirement contributions. Organize your tax documents. Set financial goals for the next quarter.</p>
  <p>By the end of 90 days, you'll have a working financial system, clear data on your habits, and a plan for growth. That puts you ahead of the vast majority of people who never build any system at all.</p>
