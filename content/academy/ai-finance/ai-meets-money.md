<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Reality</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">AI won't make you rich. But it will make you smarter about money.</h2>
  <p>Let's kill the hype immediately. AI is not going to pick winning stocks for you. It's not going to find some secret money hack. It's not going to replace a certified financial planner.</p>
  <p>What AI <em>will</em> do is handle the tedious parts of personal finance that most people avoid: tracking spending, categorizing expenses, understanding where your money goes, spotting patterns, and helping you make sense of financial concepts that feel overwhelming.</p>
  <p>Think of AI as your financial literacy tutor and organizational assistant, not your financial advisor. It helps you understand and organize. You make the decisions.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The real power:</strong> Most people don't have a money problem. They have an awareness problem. AI helps you see your finances clearly, often for the first time.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">What AI Can Do</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The legitimate use cases.</h2>
  <p><strong>Expense tracking and categorization.</strong> AI can categorize your transactions automatically, spot recurring charges you forgot about, and visualize where your money goes each month. This alone is worth the effort.</p>
  <p><strong>Budget creation and monitoring.</strong> Tell AI your income, fixed expenses, and goals. It can draft a realistic budget and help you track against it. No judgment, no shame — just numbers.</p>
  <p><strong>Financial education.</strong> Don't understand compound interest? Confused by tax brackets? AI can explain any financial concept in plain language, with examples using your actual numbers.</p>
  <p><strong>Debt analysis.</strong> AI can calculate payoff timelines, compare strategies (avalanche vs. snowball), and show you exactly how much interest you'll save with different approaches.</p>
  <p><strong>Research and comparison.</strong> AI can help you compare financial products, understand terms and conditions, and research options. It can't tell you which to choose, but it can give you the information to decide.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>What AI cannot do:</strong> Predict markets. Guarantee returns. Replace professional tax advice. Make investment decisions for you. Access your bank accounts (unless you explicitly connect a tool). Understand your full life situation the way a human advisor can.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Your Toolkit</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The AI finance toolkit.</h2>
  <p>You don't need expensive software. Here's what actually works:</p>
  <p><strong>AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini):</strong> Your primary tool. Use it for analysis, explanations, calculations, and planning. Paste in your financial data (remove sensitive details) and ask questions.</p>
  <p><strong>Spreadsheet + AI:</strong> Google Sheets or Excel with AI features. Track your finances in a spreadsheet and use AI to analyze patterns, create formulas, and generate charts. This combination is incredibly powerful.</p>
  <p><strong>Budgeting apps:</strong> Many budgeting apps now include AI features for categorization and insights. These connect to your accounts and provide automated tracking.</p>
  <p><strong>Your brain:</strong> AI provides data and analysis. You provide context, values, and decisions. Never outsource the "what matters to me" part of financial planning to a machine.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Privacy first:</strong> Be careful about what financial data you share with AI tools. Remove account numbers, SSNs, and other sensitive identifiers before pasting data. Use percentages instead of exact balances if you're uncomfortable sharing specific numbers.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">First Steps</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your first AI finance workflow.</h2>
  <p>Start here. Right now. This takes 15 minutes and it might change how you think about money.</p>
  <p><strong>Step 1:</strong> Export your last month's bank statement as a CSV or copy the transactions.</p>
  <p><strong>Step 2:</strong> Paste them into your AI assistant (remove account numbers first) and ask: "Categorize these transactions and tell me my top 5 spending categories with percentages."</p>
  <p><strong>Step 3:</strong> Ask follow-up questions: "What recurring subscriptions do I have? What's my fixed vs. discretionary spending ratio? Are there any unusual charges?"</p>
  <p><strong>Step 4:</strong> Take what you learned and decide one thing to change. Cancel a subscription. Set a spending limit for a category. Start tracking a metric you didn't know about.</p>
  <p>That's it. One export, one conversation, one action. Repeat monthly and you'll know more about your finances than most people ever will.</p>
</div>
