<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Gap</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">You weren't taught this on purpose.</h2>
  <p>Most schools don't teach personal finance. Not because it isn't important — but because the system wasn't designed for your financial literacy. The result: most adults learn about money through trial, error, and expensive mistakes.</p>
  <p>AI changes this. You now have access to a patient, non-judgmental financial tutor available 24/7. No question is too basic. No topic is too complex. And unlike a human advisor, AI has no products to sell you.</p>
  <p>Here are the concepts that matter most — the ones that, had you learned them at 18, would have changed your financial trajectory.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Learning mode:</strong> Any time you encounter a financial term or concept you don't understand, immediately ask AI. Build the habit of understanding instead of guessing. "What does amortization mean? Explain it with a simple example."
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Core Concepts</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The financial concepts that actually change lives.</h2>
  <p><strong>Inflation erodes savings.</strong> Money in a checking account loses purchasing power every year. $10,000 today buys significantly less than $10,000 ten years ago. AI can calculate exactly how much your savings lose to inflation annually. This is why investing matters.</p>
  <p><strong>Net worth is the real scorecard.</strong> Income doesn't equal wealth. Net worth = what you own minus what you owe. A person earning $200K with $300K in debt has a lower net worth than someone earning $50K with $30K in savings and no debt. Track net worth quarterly with AI.</p>
  <p><strong>Emergency funds aren't optional.</strong> 3-6 months of essential expenses, liquid and accessible. Not invested. Not tied up. This is the foundation everything else is built on. Without it, every surprise becomes a crisis.</p>
  <p><strong>Insurance is risk management.</strong> Health, auto, renters/homeowners, and eventually life and disability insurance. You're not paying for something you hope to never use — you're paying to prevent financial catastrophe. AI can explain different types and what they actually cover.</p>
  <p><strong>Lifestyle inflation is the silent killer.</strong> When income goes up, spending goes up to match. The person earning $100K who spends $95K is in worse shape than the person earning $60K who spends $45K. AI can help you track your savings rate over time.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The savings rate:</strong> What percentage of your income do you save? Most financial experts suggest 20% or more. Ask AI: "If I save [X]% of my income, how does that compound over 10, 20, and 30 years?" The answer will motivate you.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Psychology</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your brain is working against your wallet.</h2>
  <p>Money decisions aren't rational. They're emotional. Understanding the psychology helps you make better choices.</p>
  <p><strong>Present bias:</strong> Your brain values $100 today more than $200 in a year. This is why saving feels hard — it requires sacrificing present pleasure for future gain. Automation bypasses this by saving before you see the money.</p>
  <p><strong>Anchoring:</strong> The first price you see becomes your reference point. A $200 item on sale for $120 feels like a deal — even if it's only worth $80. AI can help you research actual market values before buying.</p>
  <p><strong>Social comparison:</strong> Spending to keep up with others is one of the biggest wealth destroyers. Most people displaying wealth are in debt. AI doesn't judge your spending — it just shows you the data.</p>
  <p><strong>Loss aversion:</strong> Losing $100 feels twice as painful as gaining $100 feels good. This makes people avoid investing (fear of loss) and hold losing investments too long (not wanting to "realize" the loss). Awareness is the first defense.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The 24-hour rule:</strong> For any non-essential purchase over $50, wait 24 hours. If you still want it, buy it. This simple delay eliminates most impulse purchases. AI can calculate how much you'd save annually by implementing this rule based on your spending patterns.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Spotting Scams</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">How to spot financial misinformation.</h2>
  <p>AI is excellent at fact-checking financial claims. Here are red flags to run past AI before falling for them:</p>
  <p><strong>"Guaranteed returns" above market averages.</strong> If someone promises 20% annual returns with no risk, it's either a scam or they don't understand risk. Ask AI what realistic returns look like for any given investment category.</p>
  <p><strong>"Limited time offer" pressure.</strong> Legitimate financial products don't require you to decide in 30 minutes. Urgency is a manipulation tactic. Take your time.</p>
  <p><strong>"Secret" investment strategies.</strong> There are no secrets in finance. Just concepts that are poorly understood. AI can explain any strategy in plain terms. If someone won't explain how their strategy works, walk away.</p>
  <p><strong>High-fee products with complex structures.</strong> If you can't explain the product and its fees in one sentence, you probably shouldn't buy it. Ask AI to simplify it. If even AI struggles to explain it clearly, that's a red flag.</p>
