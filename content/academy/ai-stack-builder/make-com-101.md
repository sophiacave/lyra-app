
<h3>Monitoring and Debugging</h3>
<p style="font-size:.9rem;color:#444">Regular monitoring and debugging are essential to ensure your scenarios run smoothly and efficiently.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Use Analytics:</strong> Make.com provides built-in analytics that allow you to track the performance of your scenarios. You can view metrics such as execution time, success rates, and error counts.</li>
<li><strong>Check Logs:</strong> Review the logs for each module to identify any issues or errors. The logs provide detailed information about the data flowing through your scenario and any problems that occur.</li>
<li><strong>Test Scenarios:</strong> Use the 'Run Once' feature to test your scenarios before enabling them on a schedule. This allows you to verify that everything works as expected without affecting production data.</li>
</ul>
