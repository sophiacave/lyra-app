<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Shift</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Games used to be gatekept. Not anymore.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Five years ago, making a game meant learning C++, mastering Unity, and spending months on boilerplate code before you had a single pixel moving on screen. That era is over.</p>
    <p>AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and specialized game engines now let you describe what you want and watch it materialize. You don't need a CS degree. You don't need a studio. You need an idea and the willingness to ship it.</p>
    <p>The indie game market hit $2.5 billion in 2025. Solo developers using AI are releasing polished games in days, not years. This isn't hype -- it's a structural shift in who gets to create.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Toolkit</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Your AI game dev starter pack.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Here's what's in your toolbox now:</p>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Code Generation:</strong> Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot can write game logic, physics systems, and UI code from plain English descriptions. You describe the mechanic, they write the implementation.
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Art & Assets:</strong> Midjourney, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion generate sprites, backgrounds, and UI elements. No pixel art skills required (though they help).
    </div>
    <div class="callout">
      <strong>Sound & Music:</strong> Suno, Udio, and ElevenLabs create soundtracks, effects, and voice lines. Your game sounds professional from day one.
    </div>
    <p>The magic happens when you combine these. One person with AI tools has the output of a small studio.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Real Talk</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">What AI can and can't do for your game.</h2>
  <div class="section-text">
    <p>Let's keep it honest. AI is incredible at generating code snippets, creating assets, and accelerating iteration. But it's not magic.</p>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>AI excels at:</strong> Boilerplate code, sprite generation, sound effects, level variations, bug fixing, and rapid prototyping.
    </div>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <strong>You still drive:</strong> Game feel, creative direction, player experience design, testing, and the "is this actually fun?" question that no AI can answer yet.
    </div>
    <p>Think of AI as the world's fastest intern. It can build anything you describe, but you need to know what to ask for. That's exactly what this course teaches you.</p>
  </div>
</div>
