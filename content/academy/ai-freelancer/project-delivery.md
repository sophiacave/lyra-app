<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Framework</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The AI delivery framework.</h2>
  <p>Speed without quality is just fast failure. The AI delivery framework ensures you move fast and deliver work that impresses. It has four phases:</p>
  <p><strong>Phase 1: Decompose.</strong> Break the project into atomic tasks. A 2,000-word article isn't one task — it's research, outline, intro, body sections, conclusion, editing, and SEO optimization. AI handles some of these beautifully. Others need your brain.</p>
  <p><strong>Phase 2: Classify.</strong> Label each task: AI-first (AI does the heavy lifting, you review), Human-first (you do the creative work, AI assists), or Human-only (strategy, judgment calls, client-specific decisions).</p>
  <p><strong>Phase 3: Execute.</strong> Work through AI-first tasks in batches. Then move to human-first tasks with AI assistance. Save human-only tasks for when you're sharpest.</p>
  <p><strong>Phase 4: Quality gate.</strong> Every deliverable passes through your quality check before the client sees it. No exceptions.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The 80/20 split:</strong> For most projects, AI can handle 60-80% of the execution work. Your job is the 20-40% that requires expertise, judgment, and taste.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Batch Processing</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Batch like a machine.</h2>
  <p>Context switching kills productivity. Instead of working on one task at a time across multiple projects, batch similar tasks together.</p>
  <p><strong>Research batch:</strong> Do all research for all active projects in one block. Feed AI your research questions for multiple projects simultaneously. While one response generates, review another.</p>
  <p><strong>Draft batch:</strong> Write all first drafts in one session. AI generates draft after draft while you provide prompts and light direction. You can produce 5-10 first drafts in the time it used to take to write one.</p>
  <p><strong>Edit batch:</strong> Review and refine all drafts together. Your editing brain is in gear — use it across all projects before switching modes.</p>
  <p><strong>Delivery batch:</strong> Package and send all deliverables in one go. Format, attach files, write delivery messages. Assembly-line style.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Time tracking data:</strong> Freelancers who batch-process with AI report completing projects in 30-40% of the time compared to linear task-by-task approaches.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Quality Control</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The quality gate system.</h2>
  <p>AI makes mistakes. Confidently. Your quality gate catches them before clients do.</p>
  <p><strong>Gate 1: Factual accuracy.</strong> AI hallucinates. Verify every statistic, claim, name, and date. If AI says "studies show," find the actual study or remove the claim.</p>
  <p><strong>Gate 2: Brand voice.</strong> Does the output sound like your client's brand? Or does it sound like generic AI? Read it out loud. If it sounds like a robot wrote it, rewrite the worst sections.</p>
  <p><strong>Gate 3: Scope alignment.</strong> Does the deliverable actually match what the client asked for? Re-read the brief. Compare point by point.</p>
  <p><strong>Gate 4: Polish.</strong> Formatting, grammar, consistency, file naming, and presentation. The details that signal professionalism.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Use AI to check AI.</strong> Paste your deliverable into a different AI tool and ask it to find errors, inconsistencies, or weak sections. Two AI perspectives catch more issues than one.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Knowing When</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">When to skip AI entirely.</h2>
  <p>Not every task should involve AI. Knowing when to go manual is part of the skill.</p>
  <p><strong>Skip AI when:</strong> The task requires deep original thinking. The output needs to be emotionally resonant in a very specific way. The client is paying for your unique perspective. The task is faster to do manually than to prompt, review, and edit.</p>
  <p><strong>Use AI when:</strong> The task is repeatable. It involves synthesis of existing information. Volume matters. The first draft quality is less important than iteration speed.</p>
  <p>The best AI freelancers develop an instinct for this. It comes with practice. When in doubt, try AI first — you'll know within 60 seconds if it's helping or slowing you down.</p>
</div>
