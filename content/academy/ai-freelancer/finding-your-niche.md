<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Niche Logic</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Not all niches benefit from AI equally.</h2>
  <p>Some niches get a 2x boost from AI. Others get 10x. The difference? <strong>How much of the work is repeatable, research-heavy, or content-driven.</strong></p>
  <p>High-leverage AI niches share three traits: they involve large volumes of output, they require research or data synthesis, and clients value speed. Think content marketing, data analysis, market research, technical writing, and e-commerce optimization.</p>
  <p>Low-leverage niches are those where the value is almost entirely in human judgment, relationships, or physical presence. Executive coaching, hands-on consulting, and bespoke creative direction still benefit from AI, but the multiplier is smaller.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>The sweet spot:</strong> Find a niche where you have real expertise AND where AI dramatically speeds up the boring parts of the work.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Categories</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">AI-native vs. AI-enhanced niches.</h2>
  <p><strong>AI-native niches</strong> are new categories that didn't exist before AI: prompt engineering, AI integration consulting, AI workflow design, and AI training data curation. These niches are hot right now but volatile. Competition shifts fast.</p>
  <p><strong>AI-enhanced niches</strong> are traditional freelance categories supercharged by AI: copywriting with AI research, web development with AI code generation, graphic design with AI-assisted concepting, bookkeeping with AI automation. These are more stable because the underlying demand existed before AI.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Best strategy for most people:</strong> Start AI-enhanced. Take a skill you already have and make it 5x faster with AI. AI-native niches reward those who move first, but they're riskier.
  </div>
  <p><strong>Top AI-enhanced niches right now:</strong></p>
  <p>1. <strong>Content operations</strong> — blog posts, newsletters, social content at scale. AI handles drafts, you handle strategy and voice.</p>
  <p>2. <strong>Data analysis and reporting</strong> — AI processes data, you interpret it and present findings.</p>
  <p>3. <strong>Technical documentation</strong> — AI drafts docs from codebases, you ensure accuracy and clarity.</p>
  <p>4. <strong>E-commerce optimization</strong> — product descriptions, SEO, listing optimization at scale.</p>
  <p>5. <strong>Marketing automation</strong> — email sequences, ad copy variations, A/B test analysis.</p>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Validation</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Validate before you commit.</h2>
  <p>Don't guess. Test your niche with real data before building your entire brand around it.</p>
  <p><strong>Step 1: Check demand.</strong> Search freelance platforms for your niche keywords. Are clients posting jobs? What are they paying? If there are fewer than 50 active jobs in your category, the niche might be too small.</p>
  <p><strong>Step 2: Check competition.</strong> How many freelancers are already positioned in this niche? High competition isn't bad if the market is big. Low competition in a small market means there's no money there.</p>
  <p><strong>Step 3: Test your AI advantage.</strong> Pick a sample project in your niche. Complete it with and without AI. Measure the time difference. If AI saves you less than 30% of the time, the leverage isn't strong enough.</p>
  <p><strong>Step 4: Price check.</strong> Can you charge at least $50/hr in this niche? If the market only supports $15-20/hr, AI speed won't make up for low rates.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Quick validation hack:</strong> Use AI to write three sample deliverables in your target niche. If the output is genuinely good with your editing, you've found leverage. If you're rewriting everything, AI isn't the multiplier here.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Positioning</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Niche down, then niche down again.</h2>
  <p>"AI copywriter" is too broad. "AI-powered email sequence writer for SaaS companies" is a niche. "AI-powered onboarding email specialist for B2B SaaS" is a <em>profitable</em> niche.</p>
  <p>The tighter your niche, the easier it is to: charge premium rates, build a reputation, create reusable templates, and attract the right clients without competing on price.</p>
  <p>Your niche statement should follow this formula: <strong>"I help [specific client type] with [specific deliverable] using AI to [specific advantage]."</strong></p>
  <p>Example: "I help e-commerce brands create product descriptions at scale using AI to deliver 200+ optimized listings per week."</p>
</div>
