<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Math</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">$10K/month is simpler than you think.</h2>
  <p>Let's break it down. $10,000 per month is $2,500 per week. If you work 5 days a week, that's $500 per day. If you can bill effectively for 5-6 hours a day (realistic with admin, sales, and breaks), you need an effective rate of about $85-100/hour.</p>
  <p>With AI boosting your speed, here's what that actually looks like:</p>
  <p><strong>Scenario A:</strong> 4 retainer clients at $2,500/month each. That's 20-25 hours of work per client per month with AI efficiency. Total: about 100 hours/month. Very doable.</p>
  <p><strong>Scenario B:</strong> 2 anchor retainers ($3,000/month each) plus project work filling the remaining $4,000. The retainers cover your base. Projects are upside.</p>
  <p><strong>Scenario C:</strong> Productized service generating $6,000-8,000/month plus 1-2 premium projects at $1,000-2,000 each. The productized service runs on systems. Projects add variety.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The real secret:</strong> $10K months don't come from working harder. They come from charging more per deliverable and delivering faster. AI is the engine for both.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Pipeline</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Build a pipeline that never runs dry.</h2>
  <p>Feast-and-famine cycles kill most freelance businesses. When you're busy delivering, you stop marketing. When projects end, you panic and start hustling for new work. AI breaks this cycle.</p>
  <p><strong>The 20% rule:</strong> Spend 20% of your time on business development every single week. Even when you're slammed. AI makes this possible by handling your outreach drafts, proposal writing, and follow-ups.</p>
  <p><strong>Inbound pipeline:</strong> Publish content in your niche regularly. Use AI to create LinkedIn posts, blog articles, and case studies. Position yourself as the expert. Let clients come to you.</p>
  <p><strong>Outbound pipeline:</strong> Send 5-10 personalized outreach messages per week to potential clients. AI researches the prospect and drafts the message. You review and send. It takes 30 minutes per day.</p>
  <p><strong>Referral pipeline:</strong> After every successful project, ask for referrals. Create a simple referral program — 10% of the first project from a referred client. AI can draft the referral ask and track who's referred whom.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Pipeline math:</strong> If you send 10 outreach messages per week with a 10% response rate and 30% close rate, that's roughly 1 new client per month from outbound alone. Add inbound and referrals and you'll never lack work.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Weekly Rhythm</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The weekly rhythm of a $10K freelancer.</h2>
  <p>Consistency beats hustle. Here's the weekly structure:</p>
  <p><strong>Monday:</strong> Client updates and planning. Send progress reports to all clients. Plan the week's deliverables. Review pipeline and follow up on leads.</p>
  <p><strong>Tuesday-Thursday:</strong> Deep production days. Batch your deliverable work. This is where AI shines — crank out high-quality output while AI handles the heavy lifting on research, drafts, and data processing.</p>
  <p><strong>Friday morning:</strong> Business development. Create content, send outreach, follow up on proposals. Work on your portfolio. Update case studies.</p>
  <p><strong>Friday afternoon:</strong> Admin. Invoicing, bookkeeping, tool maintenance, process improvement. Use AI to automate whatever you can.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Guard your deep work days.</strong> Tuesday through Thursday should have minimal meetings. Schedule all client calls on Monday or Friday. Protecting your production time is protecting your income.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Beyond 10K</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">From $10K to $20K without doubling your hours.</h2>
  <p>Once you've consistently hit $10K, the path to $20K isn't about working twice as hard. It's about leverage.</p>
  <p><strong>Raise rates by 20-30%.</strong> When you're at capacity, increase prices for new clients. Existing clients get a gradual increase at renewal time.</p>
  <p><strong>Add a productized service.</strong> Package your most repeatable work and sell it at scale. AI handles most of the production. You handle quality control.</p>
  <p><strong>Bring on one subcontractor.</strong> Hire someone to handle the work you're least excited about. Pay them well. Bill clients 2-3x what you pay the subcontractor.</p>
  <p><strong>Create a digital product.</strong> Templates, courses, tools, or resources that sell while you sleep. Use AI to create them. Use your existing client relationships to sell them.</p>
  <p>The progression: solo freelancer at $10K, leveraged freelancer at $15K, micro-agency owner at $20K+. Each step adds income without proportionally adding hours.</p>
</div>
