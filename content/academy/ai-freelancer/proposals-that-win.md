<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">The Problem</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">Most proposals are invisible.</h2>
  <p>The average freelance job posting gets 20-50 proposals. Clients spend about <strong>10 seconds</strong> scanning each one before deciding to read further or skip. Most freelancers write generic, template-heavy proposals that blend into the pile.</p>
  <p>AI changes this equation in two ways. First, it lets you write more proposals faster, increasing your volume. Second — and more importantly — it lets you write <em>better</em> proposals because you can research the client, personalize the pitch, and refine the language in minutes instead of hours.</p>
  <p>The goal isn't to spam proposals. It's to send fewer, sharper proposals that actually convert.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Benchmark:</strong> A good proposal win rate is 10-15%. AI-optimized proposals consistently hit 20-30% because they're more personalized and demonstrate immediate value.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Anatomy</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The five-part winning proposal.</h2>
  <p>Every winning proposal has five elements. Miss one and your conversion drops.</p>
  <p><strong>1. The hook (2 sentences).</strong> Show you understand their specific problem. Reference something from their job post or business. Never start with "Hi, I'm a freelancer with 5 years of experience."</p>
  <p><strong>2. The proof (2-3 sentences).</strong> One relevant example of similar work you've done. Include a specific result — a number, a metric, an outcome. "I wrote email sequences for a SaaS company that increased trial-to-paid conversion by 34%."</p>
  <p><strong>3. The plan (3-4 sentences).</strong> Briefly outline how you'd approach their project. This shows you've thought about it, not just copy-pasted a template. Mention your AI-enhanced process if relevant.</p>
  <p><strong>4. The deliverable (1-2 sentences).</strong> Be specific about what they'll get. "You'll receive 10 SEO-optimized blog posts, each 1,500 words, with meta descriptions and internal linking suggestions."</p>
  <p><strong>5. The close (1 sentence).</strong> Clear next step. "I'd love to discuss this on a quick 15-minute call — what works for your schedule?"</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Length rule:</strong> 150-250 words total. Anything longer and clients stop reading. AI can help you say more in fewer words.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">AI Workflow</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The 10-minute proposal system.</h2>
  <p>Here's how to use AI to produce winning proposals fast:</p>
  <p><strong>Minutes 1-3: Research.</strong> Paste the job posting into your AI assistant. Ask it to identify the client's core problem, what they value most, and what a winning response would emphasize. Also ask it to check the client's website or company for context.</p>
  <p><strong>Minutes 4-7: Draft.</strong> Give your AI the five-part structure above, your relevant experience, and the research output. Have it generate a first draft. Be specific in your prompt: "Write a 200-word proposal using this structure, referencing my experience with [specific project]."</p>
  <p><strong>Minutes 8-10: Human review.</strong> This is where you earn your money. Read every word. Does it sound like you? Does the hook actually hook? Is the proof credible? Does the plan make sense for this specific project? Edit for your voice. Remove anything generic.</p>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>Never skip the human review.</strong> Clients can smell AI-generated proposals that haven't been personalized. The AI gets you 80% there. Your expertise and voice deliver the final 20% that wins the job.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="lesson-section">
  <span class="section-label">Follow-Up</span>
  <h2 class="section-title">The follow-up system most freelancers ignore.</h2>
  <p>48 hours after sending a proposal with no response, send a follow-up. Keep it short — two sentences max. Add a small piece of value: a quick insight about their business, a relevant article, or a brief suggestion related to their project.</p>
  <p>Use AI to draft follow-ups that feel natural, not desperate. The prompt: "Write a 2-sentence follow-up to a proposal I sent 48 hours ago for [project type]. Add one small insight about [their industry]."</p>
  <p>One follow-up is enough. Two makes you persistent. Three makes you annoying. Know the line.</p>
  <div class="tip-box">
    <strong>Track everything:</strong> Use a simple spreadsheet or Notion database to track proposals sent, follow-ups, responses, and wins. You can't optimize what you don't measure.
  </div>
</div>
