## LinkedIn

**Like One Foundation: 501(c)(3) Tax-Exempt Status Approved**

I'm proud to share that Like One Foundation has received its IRS determination letter confirming 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status.

This means all donations to Like One Foundation are now tax-deductible, corporate matching programs can participate, and we're eligible for grants that require federal tax-exempt recognition.

Like One Foundation funds two missions:
• HIV cure research through partners like amfAR and UCSF
• Accessible AI education for disabled and marginalized communities

The model is simple: Like One LLC generates revenue through AI education. A percentage of every dollar flows automatically to the Foundation. As revenue scales, the giving percentage scales with it.

This milestone took two years of work. It validates the mission, the governance, and the financials.

If your organization has a matching program or you'd like to make a tax-deductible contribution to HIV cure research and accessible education: likeone.ai/support
