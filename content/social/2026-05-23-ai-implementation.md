### LinkedIn

**90% of AI projects fail. Here's what the other 10% build.**

RAND Corporation reports that 80-90% of AI projects never make it past the pilot phase. Billions of dollars spent. Nothing shipped.

The usual explanation — "bad data" or "lack of talent" — misses the real issue.

I've built production AI systems that run an entire company autonomously: grant applications, content publishing, revenue operations, client outreach. Zero employees.

The bottleneck was never data or talent. It was architecture.

Most companies treat AI as a tool — like a smarter Excel. They bolt a chatbot onto one process, it works in demo, breaks in production, and everyone goes back to the old way.

The companies that succeed build AI as infrastructure:
• Persistent memory (the AI remembers your business)
• Autonomous agents (own workflows end-to-end)
• Decision frameworks (when to act vs. escalate)
• Feedback loops (every action feeds improvement)

The model is a commodity. Everyone has access to Claude, GPT, Gemini.

The architecture is the moat.
