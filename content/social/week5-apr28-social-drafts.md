## POST 3: Authority Post — AI Myth Busting
**Publish:** Friday May 2

### X (Twitter)
"AI will replace my employees" — No, it won't.

AI replaces TASKS, not people.

The meeting notes nobody wants to take? AI.
The data entry everyone dreads? AI.
The follow-up emails that slip through cracks? AI.

Your people do the thinking. AI does the tedious.

That's the whole framework.

### LinkedIn
I keep hearing the same fear from business owners:

"If I automate with AI, I'll need fewer people."

After working with dozens of businesses on AI adoption, here's what actually happens:

Your team stops doing work they hate.
They start doing work that matters.
Output goes up. Burnout goes down.

AI doesn't replace employees. It replaces the worst parts of their jobs.

The companies that frame AI as a replacement tool get resistance. The companies that frame it as a "never do the boring stuff again" tool get adoption.

Framing is everything.
