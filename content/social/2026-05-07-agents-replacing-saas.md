### LinkedIn

The SaaS model was built on a simple premise: cloud software, monthly fee. It worked for two decades.

But here's the dirty secret — most SaaS is shelf-ware. Companies use less than 40% of the features they pay for.

AI agents flip this entirely. Instead of 200 features hoping you find the 3 you need, an agent asks: what do you need done? Then it does it.

This isn't theory. It's shipping right now in customer support, sales development, back office ops, and content.

The companies figuring this out first aren't getting a marginal advantage. They're operating at a fundamentally different cost structure.

Three things to do today:
1. Audit your SaaS stack — what's giving capabilities vs. doing work poorly?
2. Start with boring automation (data entry, email triage, reporting)
3. Build the prompt engineering + agent design skill in-house

The new literacy isn't optional.
